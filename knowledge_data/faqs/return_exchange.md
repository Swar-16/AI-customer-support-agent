# Return & Exchange FAQ

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Policy Domain:** RETURN & EXCHANGE  
**Audience:** Customers and Customer Support

---

### Basic Eligibility

#### 1. How long do I have to return something?

You generally have **30 calendar days from the confirmed delivery date** to request a return for an eligible item.

For orders containing multiple shipments, each item is evaluated based on its own delivery date.

The item should normally be unused, undamaged, and in saleable condition, with its included accessories and packaging where reasonably available.

#### 2. Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?

Yes. Eligible items can generally be exchanged **within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery**.

The exchange is subject to product availability and the item's condition. If the replacement costs more, you must pay the price difference before it is dispatched. If it costs less, the difference is normally refunded to the original payment method after the exchange is approved.

#### 3. What if I already opened or tried the product?

Opening or reasonably inspecting an item does not automatically make it ineligible.

However, products generally need to be **unused and in saleable condition**. Items showing wear, stains, odors, alterations, damage, or misuse may be rejected.

If the item is defective or arrived damaged, tell Customer Support because defect and damage cases are handled differently from ordinary change-of-mind returns.

### Starting a Return

#### 4. How do I actually return my order?

Follow these steps:

1. Confirm that the item is within the **30-calendar-day** return window.
2. Submit a return request with your order number and reason.
3. Obtain an **RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization)** if one is required.
4. Package the correct item securely with required accessories.
5. Use the authorized return label or carrier instructions.
6. Keep your tracking number until the refund or exchange is complete.

If an RMA is issued, it normally remains valid for **14 calendar days**.

> **Important:** Do not send a return to an address copied from an old order or another source. Use only the return instructions provided for your specific RMA.

#### 5. I lost my order number. Can I still return the item?

Possibly. Customer Support can attempt to locate the purchase using verified account information or other transaction details.

A refund normally requires that the Company can verify the original transaction. Refunds are generally sent to the **original payment method**.

### Exceptions & Troubleshooting

#### 6. What items can't be returned?

Some products are generally **non-refundable and non-exchangeable**, including:

- Final-sale or explicitly designated clearance merchandise.
- Personalized or custom-made products.
- Certain hygiene-sensitive products after opening.
- Certain consumables after opening or use.
- Gift cards or stored-value products.
- Certain digitally delivered products.

These restrictions may not apply when the item is defective, damaged on arrival, incorrectly shipped, or applicable law provides a different right.

#### 7. My item arrived damaged. What should I do?

Report the damage **within 7 calendar days of delivery** whenever possible.

Provide your order number and clear photographs of the damaged product. Photos of the external and internal packaging may also be requested.

If the damage is validated, the Company may provide a replacement, exchange, or refund and may provide a prepaid return label when the item must be returned.

#### 8. The product is defective. Is that treated like a normal return?

Not necessarily.

A suspected manufacturing defect is evaluated separately from a customer-remorse return. Support may ask for photographs, video, serial numbers, or other information to verify the problem.

If the defect is confirmed or reasonably established, the Company may authorize a **replacement, exchange, repair, or refund**, depending on the product and circumstances.

#### 9. I'm past the 30-day deadline. Can you still take it back?

Late returns are **normally not approved**, but exceptions may be reviewed.

Examples that may justify escalation include a confirmed Company system error, a material Company/carrier shipping delay, or a product defect discovered shortly after the standard window.

Customer Support cannot guarantee approval of a late-return exception unless the circumstances create a legally protected right.

### Refunds, Fees & Timelines

#### 10. When will I get my refund?

Once your return arrives, eligible merchandise is normally inspected within **3 business days**.

After approval, the Company generally initiates the refund within **2 business days**. Your bank, card issuer, or payment provider may then take approximately **5–7 business days** to post the money.

So the return-processing timeline and the bank-posting timeline are separate.

If the Company confirms that the refund was issued and **7 business days** have passed without the funds appearing, contact Customer Support with your refund confirmation information.

#### 11. Do I have to pay for return shipping?

It depends on why you're returning the item.

| Reason | Typical Shipping Treatment |
|---|---|
| Changed your mind | Customer-paid |
| Wrong item shipped | Company-paid |
| Validated defective product | Company-paid |
| Validated arrival damage | Company-paid |
| Company fulfillment error | Company-paid |
| International customer-remorse return | Customer-paid |

The exact treatment can vary based on product-specific or contractual terms.

Standard consumer returns generally have **no restocking fee**. Certain commercial, bulk, custom, or contract-specific returns may have a processing/restocking deduction of up to **15%**, when disclosed and legally permitted.

#### 12. I returned an international order. Why is my refund different from what I paid?

International refunds are normally processed in the **original transaction currency**.

Your bank may convert that amount into your local currency using its own exchange rate. Foreign transaction fees, issuer fees, or exchange-rate changes can therefore cause the amount shown in your account to differ from the original local-currency amount.

Unless otherwise required by law or stated in your purchase terms, international duties, taxes, customs charges, and brokerage fees may be **non-refundable**.
