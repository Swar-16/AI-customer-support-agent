# ACCOUNT FAQ

> **Applies From:** 28 August 2026  
> **Audience:** Customers using standard consumer or small-business accounts  
> **Related Policy:** Account Policy v1.0

---

### Basic Eligibility

#### 1. How old do I have to be to create an account?

You generally need to be **18 years or older** to create a standard Company account.

Some separately supported services may have different age rules where legally valid authorization is available. Do not create an account using someone else's identity or date of birth.

If the Company cannot legally provide a service in your country or region, account access or particular features may also be unavailable.

---

#### 2. What information do I need to sign up?

A standard account normally requires a valid **email address** and the information shown during registration.

You may also be asked for a phone number, payment information, identity information, or business details depending on the product and security requirements.

Email verification may be required before the account or certain features are fully activated. Verification links generally remain valid for **24 hours**.

---

#### 3. Why haven't I received my verification email?

Verification emails are normally sent within approximately **5 minutes**, but delivery can take longer because of email-provider delays, spam filtering, or security rate limits.

Try these steps:

1. Check spam, junk, promotions, and quarantine folders.
2. Confirm that the email address was entered correctly.
3. Request a new verification message.
4. Wait for the newest message rather than using an expired link.
5. Contact Support if repeated attempts fail.

A verification link generally expires after **24 hours**.

---

### Login, Password & Security

#### 4. I forgot my password. How can I get back in?

Use the **Forgot Password** option on the sign-in screen.

A password-reset message is normally sent within approximately **5 minutes** when the account is eligible for automated recovery. The reset link generally remains valid for **30 minutes**.

After opening the link:

1. Create a new password.
2. Complete any requested security verification.
3. Sign in again.
4. Review recent account activity if you suspect someone else accessed the account.

For security, existing sessions may be signed out after a password reset.

---

#### 5. I lost access to the email on my account. Can you change it for me?

Possibly, but the Company must first establish that you own the account.

Contact Customer Support and provide the requested account information. You may be asked for transaction details, previous account information, identity evidence, or other ownership documentation.

Manual account-recovery reviews generally target **1–3 business days**. Complex ownership or security cases may take longer.

> **Important:** Knowing the old email address, account name, or an order number does **not automatically prove account ownership**.

---

#### 6. Someone may have gotten into my account. What should I do right now?

Act immediately.

1. Change your password if you can still sign in.
2. Sign out unknown sessions or devices, if that option is available.
3. Enable or re-enable multi-factor authentication.
4. Review recent account activity and transactions.
5. Contact Support if you see unauthorized activity.

The Company may temporarily restrict the account while Security Operations investigates. A manual security investigation commonly targets **3–7 business days**, although complex cases can take longer.

Never give a support representative your password, one-time code, or MFA recovery code.

---

### Account Changes & Administration

#### 7. How do I change my email address or other account details?

Sign in and open **Account Settings**.

For a normal profile change:

1. Select the field you want to change.
2. Enter the new information.
3. Complete any requested verification.
4. Save the change.
5. Confirm the new information is displayed correctly.

Changing an email address may require verification of the new address. A verification link generally expires after **24 hours**.

Security-sensitive changes may trigger password, MFA, or identity verification.

---

#### 8. Can I have more than one account?

Having multiple accounts is not automatically permitted for every use case.

You must not create additional accounts to bypass:

- Account suspensions or restrictions.
- Promotion limits.
- Subscription limits.
- Identity checks.
- Payment controls.
- Geographic restrictions.
- Fraud-prevention measures.

Accounts created for circumvention may be restricted or suspended.

Business or enterprise customers may have multiple authorized user accounts under an organization, subject to the applicable plan or agreement.

---

### Closing, Restrictions & Special Cases

#### 9. I want to delete my account. Will everything disappear immediately?

You can request account closure through supported account settings or Customer Support.

Standard account closure generally targets completion within **24 hours**, but immediate deletion may not be possible when the account has an unresolved refund, chargeback, open order, outstanding balance, fraud investigation, legal dispute, or other required hold.

Also, deleting an account does not automatically erase information that the Company is legally required to retain.

Closing an account does not automatically cancel every separate subscription or contractual service unless its applicable terms provide for that result.

---

#### 10. I was locked out or suspended, but I didn't do anything wrong. What can I do?

Contact Customer Support and provide the account identifier, a description of what happened, relevant dates, and any supporting evidence.

Security restrictions may be triggered by unusual login activity, suspected account takeover, payment fraud, automated abuse, repeated failed authentication, or other risk signals.

The Company may ask you to complete additional verification before restoring access.

> **Security comes first:** Support may not remove an account-security restriction merely because a customer requests immediate access.

---

### Processing Timelines & Money

#### 11. Do I have to pay to create, maintain, recover, or close my account?

There is generally **no standard fee** just to create, maintain, recover, or close a standard account.

A specific paid service, subscription, enterprise agreement, transaction, or other commercial product may have its own charges.

You should not be asked to pay a special "password recovery fee" simply because you forgot your password.

---

#### 12. What happens to my refunds, credits, or money if I close my account?

Closing an account does not automatically cancel a pending refund or convert a non-refundable credit into cash.

A pending financial transaction may continue to be processed according to the applicable payment, refund, subscription, or order terms.

Refunds are generally returned through the applicable original payment method and may be subject to payment-network, processor, currency, or legal limitations.

If you have an unresolved refund or payment dispute, resolve that matter before requesting account closure where practical.
