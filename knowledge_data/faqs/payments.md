# Payment FAQ

## Basic Payment Eligibility

### 1. What payment methods can I use?

The Company may support **credit cards, debit cards, UPI, digital wallets, and bank transfers**, depending on your billing country and account configuration.

The payment methods available to you are the methods displayed during checkout or on your invoice.

Some payment methods may not be available for international transactions, enterprise accounts, or certain currencies.

### 2. When is my payment actually considered successful?

Your payment is considered successfully completed when the Company or its authorized payment processor confirms it as **Captured**, **Successful**, or an equivalent completed status.

A transaction showing as **Pending** or merely showing a temporary authorization on your card does not necessarily mean the Company has received the money.

If you are unsure, check the payment status before trying again.

> **Do not repeatedly submit a payment while the original transaction is still pending.** Wait at least **15 minutes** for standard payment reconciliation unless the checkout page instructs you otherwise.

### 3. My card payment was declined. What should I do?

First, check whether the payment was actually debited.

If it was **not debited**:

1. Check the card number, expiry date, billing information, and available credit.
2. Complete any required 3-D Secure or OTP authentication.
3. Retry the payment once.
4. If it fails again, use another supported payment method or contact your card issuer.

The Company cannot override a decline made by your bank or card issuer.

## Pending, Failed & Duplicate Payments

### 4. My account was charged, but the payment says "failed." What now?

**Do not pay again immediately.**

A bank or card issuer can show a debit or authorization even when the payment has not yet been successfully captured by the Company.

Wait at least **30 minutes** for the payment status to reconcile, particularly for UPI transactions.

If the payment remains failed or the amount remains debited after the applicable processing period, contact Payments Support with:

- Invoice/order number
- Transaction/reference ID
- Amount and currency
- Payment date and approximate time
- Payment method
- Bank/payment confirmation or screenshot

### 5. I accidentally paid the same invoice twice. Can you help?

Yes. Do **not** make another payment.

Send Payments Support both transaction/reference IDs and the relevant invoice number.

Payments Operations will determine whether both transactions were successfully captured and whether they represent a duplicate payment. Confirmed duplicate payments are handled under the Company's applicable refund or account-credit procedures.

### 6. My UPI payment is stuck or still pending. How long should I wait?

If your bank account was debited but the Company still shows the payment as pending, wait at least **30 minutes** before taking further action.

Do not initiate another payment during this period unless the system explicitly tells you to do so.

If the transaction remains unresolved after the reconciliation period, contact Payments Support with your UPI/bank transaction reference.

### 7. I tried paying several times and now payment isn't working at all. Why?

Repeated failed attempts can trigger fraud-prevention controls.

As a standard safeguard, **more than 5 failed payment attempts within 30 minutes** may cause a temporary payment restriction. The restriction can last up to **24 hours**.

Do not attempt to bypass the restriction by repeatedly changing cards or creating duplicate accounts.

If the restriction appears to have been triggered incorrectly, contact Payments Support.

## Processing Timelines & Bank Transfers

### 8. How long does a bank transfer take to show up?

Typical processing times are:

| Payment Method | Typical Time |
|---|---:|
| Card | Usually minutes |
| UPI | Usually minutes |
| Domestic bank transfer | **1–2 business days** |
| International bank transfer | **2–5 business days** |
| Enterprise invoice | According to contract |

International transfers can take up to **7 business days** in cases involving intermediary banks, compliance checks, weekends, or banking delays.

If your transfer exceeds the applicable timeframe, provide the bank transaction reference and proof of payment so Payments Operations can investigate.

### 9. I sent a bank transfer, but my invoice still says unpaid. What should I do?

First, verify that you transferred the **exact invoiced amount** to the official bank details shown on your Company invoice.

Then confirm that the invoice number or required payment reference was included with the transfer.

If the payment cannot be automatically matched, Payments Operations may request proof of payment. Manual reconciliation generally takes **1 business day** after sufficient evidence is received.

> **Never send money to bank details supplied by an unverified third party.** If bank details appear to have changed unexpectedly, verify them through an official Company channel before transferring funds.

## Currency, Fees & International Payments

### 10. Why is the amount on my bank statement different from the amount I saw?

International payments can involve currency conversion.

The Company displays the applicable transaction amount and currency before payment whenever practicable. However, your bank, card issuer, wallet provider, or intermediary financial institution may apply its own exchange rate or foreign-exchange fee.

As a result, the amount appearing on your bank statement may differ from an indicative converted amount shown during checkout.

### 11. Are there extra fees for paying internationally?

The Company does not impose a standard payment-processing fee unless one is specifically disclosed at checkout, on your invoice, or in your commercial agreement.

However, your bank or financial institution may charge:

- Foreign-exchange fees
- Cross-border transaction fees
- Intermediary-bank fees
- Bank-transfer fees
- Other financial-institution charges

Those third-party charges are generally outside the Company's control.

### 12. Can I use a corporate or enterprise payment arrangement instead of paying immediately?

Possibly.

Enterprise customers may have contract-specific payment terms such as **Net 15, Net 30, Net 45, or Net 60**, purchase-order billing, or milestone-based payments.

If you have an executed enterprise agreement, its payment terms control where they conflict with the standard Payment Policy.

If your account does not have approved invoice terms, you must use the payment method and deadline presented on the applicable invoice or checkout page.
