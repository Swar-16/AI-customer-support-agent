# Cancellation FAQ

> **Policy Version:** 1.0  
> **Effective Date:** 2026-08-28  
> **Audience:** Customers and Customer Support  
> **Domain:** CANCELLATION

---

## Basic Eligibility

### 1. Can I cancel my order?

Usually, yes, if you submit the request **within 24 hours of order confirmation** and fulfillment has not started.

Sign in to your Company account, open the relevant order, select **Cancel** or **Request Cancellation**, and confirm the request.

If the order has already entered irreversible fulfillment or has shipped, standard cancellation may no longer be available. You may instead need to use the applicable return or refund process.

**Important:** A cancellation request is not considered completed until you receive a cancellation confirmation.

---

### 2. I just placed my order. Can I cancel it right now?

If your order is still within the applicable cancellation window, request cancellation immediately.

For standard orders, the normal rule is **within 24 hours of order confirmation**, provided fulfillment has not started.

The sooner you submit the request, the greater the chance that the order can be stopped before fulfillment or shipment.

If the website does not allow cancellation, contact Customer Support immediately and provide your order ID. If a Company technical problem prevented a timely cancellation attempt, the original attempted timestamp may be reviewed.

---

### 3. Is cancelling an order the same thing as getting a refund?

**No.**

Cancellation and refund eligibility are separate decisions.

For example, an order may qualify for cancellation but have a deduction because fulfillment has already begun. Conversely, a transaction that cannot be cancelled may still qualify for a refund or other remedy under applicable return, consumer-protection, or contractual rules.

The cancellation confirmation should identify the expected financial outcome where one can be determined at the time of cancellation.

---

## Subscriptions and Recurring Charges

### 4. How do I cancel my subscription?

Sign in to your account and:

1. Open **Subscriptions**.
2. Select the subscription.
3. Choose **Cancel**.
4. Review the displayed cancellation terms.
5. Confirm the cancellation.
6. Save the cancellation confirmation/reference number.

For most subscriptions, cancellation before the next renewal prevents the next recurring charge.

Unless your subscription terms say otherwise, cancelling does not necessarily end the already-paid service period immediately. You may continue using the service until the end of that paid period.

---

### 5. I forgot to cancel and my subscription just renewed. Can you undo the charge?

Contact Customer Support as soon as possible.

An unintended renewal submitted within **24 hours after renewal** may be routed for billing review where the applicable subscription terms allow discretionary review.

A review is not an automatic refund guarantee. The outcome can depend on the subscription type, usage, promotional terms, contract, payment status, and applicable law.

If a refund is approved, the refund is normally initiated within **1 business day** after approval.

---

### 6. Will cancelling my subscription immediately stop my access?

Not necessarily.

For subscriptions that are paid in advance, cancellation generally stops the **next renewal** while access continues through the end of the current paid period.

Some subscriptions, enterprise agreements, minimum-term plans, or service-specific products may have different rules.

Always check the cancellation confirmation for the exact effective cancellation date.

## Troubleshooting & Exceptions

### 7. Why don't I see a Cancel button?

A Cancel option may be unavailable because:

- the cancellation window has expired;
- fulfillment has already started;
- the order has shipped;
- the product is non-cancellable;
- the service has already started;
- the account requires additional verification;
- the transaction is under a security or payment review; or
- your enterprise contract requires a different cancellation procedure.

If you believe you should still be eligible, contact Customer Support before the applicable deadline and provide your order or subscription ID.

---

### 8. Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?

Generally, **no**. Once a physical order has been handed to the carrier, standard cancellation is normally unavailable.

You may need to:

1. Receive the shipment.
2. Initiate an eligible return.
3. Follow the return instructions provided by Company.
4. Wait for the return and refund assessment.

If the package is returned to sender or refused, the financial outcome is determined under the applicable return/refund rules.

---

### 9. Can I cancel a customized or personalized product?

Possibly, but timing is critical.

A customized product may normally be cancelled **before production begins**.

Once production has started, cancellation may be rejected or may incur a cancellation charge of **up to 25% of the affected order value**, depending on the product terms and circumstances.

The production-start timestamp recorded in Company systems is normally used when determining eligibility.

---

### 10. Can I cancel only one item from a larger order?

Often, yes.

Eligible items in a multi-item order may be cancelled individually. However, cancelling part of an order can change bundle pricing, promotional discounts, shipping charges, or other financial terms.

For example, if a discount required purchasing three items and one item is cancelled, the remaining items may be repriced according to the applicable promotion terms.

The final cancellation/refund amount is calculated using the applicable transaction rules rather than simply dividing the original order total by the number of items.

## Fees and Processing Timelines

### 11. Do I have to pay a cancellation fee?

It depends on when and what you are cancelling.

| Situation | Typical Fee |
|---|---:|
| Eligible cancellation before fulfillment | **$0** |
| Subscription cancelled before renewal | **$0** |
| Cancellation after fulfillment begins | May be **up to 10%** |
| Customized product after production begins | May be **up to 25%** |
| Enterprise early termination | According to signed contract |

The exact fee, if any, should be disclosed before an eligible cancellation is finalized when the system supports advance disclosure.

---

### 12. How long does it take to get my money back after cancellation?

Once an approved refund is initiated, typical processing times are:

| Refund Method | Typical Time |
|---|---:|
| Credit/debit card | **5–7 business days** |
| Bank transfer | **3–7 business days** |
| Digital wallet | **1–5 business days** |
| Company account credit | Usually same day |
| International refund | **5–10 business days** |

These timeframes begin when the refund is initiated, not necessarily when you first request cancellation.

Banks, card networks, payment providers, weekends, and public holidays can add additional processing time.

> **If your refund has been marked as completed by Company but the money has not appeared after the stated timeframe, contact Customer Support with your cancellation or refund reference number.**

---

## Special Circumstances

### 13. I paid in another currency. Will I get exactly the same amount back?

The approved refund is generally based on the original transaction and applicable refund rules.

However, your bank or card issuer may display a different amount in your local currency because exchange rates can change between the original charge and the refund.

Company generally does not compensate for third-party foreign-exchange differences or bank fees unless required by applicable law or expressly approved.

---

### 14. What if I tried to cancel before the deadline but your website wasn't working?

Contact Customer Support and provide:

- order or subscription ID;
- approximate time of your cancellation attempt;
- screenshot or error message, if available;
- any cancellation request/reference number you received.

If Company systems prevented a timely cancellation, the case can be reviewed using available system records.

When the evidence confirms a qualifying attempt before the deadline, Customer Operations may evaluate the request using the **original attempted cancellation timestamp** rather than the later manual-support timestamp.

---

### 15. My cancellation was rejected, but I think it should have been accepted. What can I do?

Ask Customer Support to review the cancellation decision.

Include:

1. Your order/subscription ID.
2. Your cancellation reference, if available.
3. The date and approximate time you attempted cancellation.
4. The reason you believe the request qualified.
5. Screenshots, confirmation emails, or other relevant evidence.

Customer Operations normally responds within **2 business days** after escalation.

Complex billing, contractual, compliance, or enterprise cases may take longer and can be escalated to the appropriate specialist team.

---

### 16. Can I cancel while my payment or account is under security review?

A cancellation or refund may be delayed while a transaction is subject to fraud, account-security, payment, or compliance review.

You may be asked to complete additional identity verification.

For security reasons, Company representatives cannot disclose confidential fraud-detection rules, risk scores, or internal security thresholds.

Once the review is complete, Company will determine the applicable cancellation or refund outcome under the relevant policy and transaction terms.
