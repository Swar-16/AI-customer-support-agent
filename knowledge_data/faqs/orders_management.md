# ORDER MANAGEMENT — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

## Basic Eligibility

### 1. Can I change my order after I place it?

Usually, yes. Company provides a **60-minute grace period** starting when your order is successfully submitted and payment is authorized.

During that window, you can generally request changes to your:

- Shipping address
- Size
- Color
- Quantity
- Items
- Shipping method

However, **60 minutes is the maximum change window, not a guarantee**. Your order can become locked earlier if it enters fulfillment, is allocated to a warehouse, starts picking or packing, reaches a carrier cutoff, or requires fraud/security processing.

A change is complete only after Company confirms it.

### 2. I just ordered the wrong size/color. Can you fix it right now?

Yes, provided the order has not entered a fulfillment-locked state and the replacement variant is available.

Contact Company immediately and provide:

1. Your order number
2. The item/SKU you want changed
3. The new size or color
4. Any requested quantity change

The best opportunity to make the change is **within 60 minutes of placing the order**.

> **Do not assume the change worked simply because you requested it.** Wait for the revised order confirmation.

### 3. Can I change my shipping address?

An address change is generally possible **within the 60-minute grace period**, provided the order is still eligible for modification.

The new address must pass:

- Address validation
- Carrier serviceability checks
- Fraud/risk screening
- Any international or export restrictions

If the order has already entered fulfillment, Company may be unable to change it.

For international orders, changing the destination country generally requires **cancelling the original order and placing a new order** because taxes, duties, customs documentation, and shipping charges can change.

## Order Statuses & Timing

### 4. What does "Pending" mean?

**Pending** means Company has received the order, but one or more initial checks are still incomplete.

Examples include:

- Payment confirmation
- Inventory allocation
- Fraud/security screening
- Address validation

A Pending order is generally easier to modify than an order that has entered **Processing**.

### 5. What does "Processing" mean? Can I still change it?

**Processing** means fulfillment activity has started. Your inventory may have been allocated and the warehouse may be picking, packing, or preparing the shipment.

At this stage, changes are **not guaranteed**.

Customer support may attempt a cancellation or modification, but the request can fail if the warehouse has progressed too far.

If the change cannot be made, you may need to use the applicable return or exchange process after delivery.

### 6. What do "Shipped" and "Delivered" mean?

**Shipped** means your package has been handed to, or formally released for handoff to, the designated carrier and a shipment/tracking event has been created.

**Delivered** means the carrier has recorded the shipment as delivered to the designated address or an approved delivery point.

Once an order is **Shipped**, Company cannot guarantee that the address, items, or delivery instructions can be changed.

Once an order is **Delivered**, normal order modification is no longer available; any issue must be handled through the applicable post-delivery process.

### 7. How fast will my order move from Processing to Shipped?

For standard orders, Company generally targets warehouse processing within **1–2 business days** after payment and validation are complete.

Expedited orders may be processed the same business day when:

- The order is placed before the applicable cutoff
- Inventory is available
- Payment and fraud checks are completed
- No operational restriction applies

After shipment, standard domestic delivery generally takes **3–7 business days**.

International delivery is typically **5–12 business days after shipment**, excluding customs delays.

## Troubleshooting & Exceptions

### 8. I asked for a change within the 60 minutes, but it was already too late. Why?

The **60-minute period is an outer limit**. An order can become locked before the 60 minutes expire.

For example, the warehouse may begin processing an order immediately because of:

- Same-day or expedited shipping requirements
- Warehouse workload
- Automated inventory allocation
- Carrier pickup schedules
- Fraud screening
- Product-specific fulfillment rules

When that happens, the order-management system may prevent further edits even though less than 60 minutes have elapsed.

### 9. My order is on hold. What does that mean?

**On Hold** means Company needs additional review before the order can proceed.

Common reasons include:

- Payment issues
- Address inconsistencies
- Unusual transaction activity
- Fraud-prevention checks
- Inventory problems
- International shipping restrictions

A security or fraud review may take **up to 2 business days**.

Company may contact you for additional verification. Do not provide a full card number, CVV, password, or one-time authentication code through customer support.

### 10. Can I add another item to an order after checkout?

You can request an additional item while the order is still eligible for modification.

Company will check:

1. Whether the order is still within the **60-minute grace period** or otherwise modifiable
2. Whether the item is in stock
3. Whether additional payment authorization is required
4. Whether shipping, tax, or promotional pricing changes

Adding an item is not guaranteed once fulfillment has started.

If the original order is already locked, you may need to place a separate order.

## Processing Timelines & Money

### 11. Will changing my order change the price or shipping cost?

It can.

For example:

- A more expensive size, color, or product may require additional payment.
- Removing an item may reduce your order total.
- Changing the address can change shipping charges.
- International destination changes may affect duties and taxes.
- Removing an item can cause a promotional discount to disappear.

There is normally **no separate order-modification fee** for eligible changes made before fulfillment lock, but you remain responsible for any genuine difference in product, tax, shipping, or other transaction charges.

### 12. When will I get a refund if changing my order makes the total lower?

For an approved order modification that creates a refund, Company ordinarily initiates the refund within **2 business days**.

After initiation, the financial institution or payment processor controls when the money appears in your account. Card refunds commonly take **3–10 business days** to post.

For international purchases, the amount ultimately received in local currency can differ because exchange rates may have changed between the original transaction and the refund.

### 13. What should I do if I accidentally placed two orders?

Contact Company immediately with both order numbers.

If the duplicate order has not entered fulfillment lock, Company may be able to cancel it.

If it has already entered fulfillment, cancellation may no longer be possible. In that situation, the order may need to be handled through the applicable return process.

**Do not place another replacement order solely because you are waiting for support unless necessary**, as this can create additional inventory and payment complications.

### 14. What information do I need to dispute an order problem?

Start with:

- Your order number
- Order confirmation
- A description of the problem
- Relevant tracking information
- Photos of packaging or items when applicable
- Payment transaction details that do not expose sensitive authentication data

Company may ask for additional documentation depending on the issue.

Escalated cases are generally assigned a response target of **1 business day** for Order Operations and up to **2 business days** for specialist teams such as Payments, Fraud, Logistics, or Compliance.
