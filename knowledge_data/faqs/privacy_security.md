# Privacy, Security & Data Protection FAQ

## Basic Eligibility

### 1. What personal information does the Company collect about me?

The Company may collect information needed to operate your account and provide its services, including your **name, contact details, transaction information, device information, usage information, support communications, and security-related information**.

The exact categories depend on which products and features you use.

The Company applies data-minimization principles and does not require information that is unnecessary for the relevant purpose.

---

### 2. Can I ask the Company to show me the personal data it has about me?

Yes. You can submit a verified **data access request**.

You generally need to provide your account identifier, explain the information you are requesting, and complete reasonable identity verification.

After verification, the Company generally targets completion **within 30 calendar days**, or within a shorter period where applicable law requires it.

A data export may be delivered in **CSV, JSON, or another machine-readable format** where technically feasible.

The Company may withhold information when disclosure would violate another person's privacy, reveal protected confidential information, or otherwise be prohibited by law.

---

### 3. I want all my data deleted. How do I do that?

Submit a verified **deletion request** through the Company's privacy channel or supported account settings.

The usual process is:

1. Submit the request.
2. Complete identity verification.
3. The Company checks whether any information must legally be retained.
4. Eligible information is deleted or irreversibly anonymized.
5. You receive a completion notice.

The Company generally targets completion **within 30 calendar days after successful verification**.

> **Important:** Deletion does not necessarily mean every record disappears immediately. Tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, legal, or regulatory records may need to be retained.

---

## Privacy Controls & Choices

### 4. How do I stop marketing emails? I don't want them anymore.

Use the **unsubscribe** link in the marketing message or update your communication preferences in your account.

Marketing opt-out requests are normally processed within **24 hours**.

A small number of messages already queued for delivery may arrive before the suppression takes effect.

Opting out of marketing does **not** disable necessary transactional or security communications, such as password-reset alerts, billing notices, service messages, or important account-security notifications.

---

### 5. Can I withdraw my consent?

Yes, where the relevant processing is based on consent.

You can change applicable privacy or communication preferences, and the Company generally aims to reflect those changes **within 72 hours**.

Withdrawal does not:

- Undo processing that occurred before withdrawal;
- Automatically stop processing required by law;
- Necessarily stop processing needed to perform your contract;
- Guarantee that optional features dependent on that consent will remain available.

---

### 6. Can I correct information that is wrong?

Yes.

You can usually correct ordinary profile information directly through account settings. For information requiring manual review, such as certain billing, legal, or compliance records, submit a verified correction request.

Routine manual corrections generally target completion within **1–5 business days**.

If a record must legally be preserved, the Company may retain the original record while recording the corrected information.

---

## Security & Account Protection

### 7. I think someone hacked my account. What should I do right now?

Treat a suspected account takeover as urgent.

Immediately:

1. Change your password from a trusted device.
2. Sign out other sessions if that option is available.
3. Revoke unknown API keys or connected applications.
4. Enable **multi-factor authentication (MFA)**.
5. Review recent account activity.
6. Contact the Company's approved security/support channel.
7. Include the approximate time of the suspicious activity and relevant screenshots or device information.

For a qualifying high-severity security report, the Company targets initial triage **within 4 hours**.

Do not send your password or MFA code to support.

---

### 8. Does the Company know my password or MFA code?

No. Company support personnel should **never ask you to provide your password or one-time MFA code** through an ordinary support interaction.

Passwords should be protected using industry-standard cryptographic password-hashing controls rather than reversible plaintext storage.

> **Security Warning:** Anyone asking you to disclose your password, authentication code, private key, or payment security code while claiming to be Company support should be treated as suspicious.

---

### 9. Is my payment card information stored by the Company?

The Company generally does not retain full payment-card security codes.

Payment transactions may be handled by approved payment processors. Company systems may retain limited information such as **masked payment details, transaction identifiers, billing status, and accounting records**.

Never put a full card number or card security code into an ordinary support ticket or chat message.

## Data Retention, Exceptions & International Issues

### 10. How long does the Company keep my information after I close my account?

There is no single retention period for every type of data.

Typical targets include:

| Information | Typical Retention |
|---|---:|
| Active account profile | While the account is active |
| Inactive account data | Up to **24 months** after meaningful activity |
| Support records | Up to **24 months** after closure |
| Transaction/billing records | Up to **7 years** |
| Security/access logs | Generally **12 months** |
| Fraud-related records | Generally **5 years** from the last relevant event |
| Backups | Typically **35–90 days** |

Legal, fraud, security, litigation, tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements can require longer retention.

---

### 11. Why wasn't everything deleted immediately when I requested deletion?

Your deletion request may be subject to a lawful exception.

For example, the Company may need to retain information for:

- **Tax or accounting records**
- Fraud prevention
- Security investigations
- Regulatory obligations
- Legal claims
- Litigation or a legal hold
- Contractual recordkeeping

Also, information removed from active production systems may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until the normal **35–90 day backup lifecycle** completes.

That does not mean the Company is continuing to use the backup data for ordinary product operations.

---

### 12. Is my data transferred to other countries?

It may be.

The Company may use global cloud, support, payment, analytics, security, and other service providers, which can result in processing outside your home country.

Where international-transfer rules apply, the Company uses an appropriate legal mechanism, such as an **adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, or another legally recognized safeguard**.

The specific mechanism can depend on your country, account type, and applicable law.

## Troubleshooting, Disputes & Escalation

### 13. My privacy request has been denied. What can I do?

You can appeal the decision.

Include:

- Your privacy request ID;
- The decision you disagree with;
- Why you believe the decision should be reconsidered;
- Any additional supporting information.

The Privacy & Security Office may perform a secondary review.

A request can be limited or denied when the Company is legally required to retain information, disclosure would affect another person's rights, the request is fraudulent, or another statutory exception applies.

Where available, you may also have the right to contact a relevant data-protection or privacy regulator.

---

### 14. Someone else's information appeared in my account. What should I do?

Do not forward, publish, download, or otherwise distribute information belonging to another person.

Report the issue immediately through the Company's security/privacy channel and provide:

- Your account identifier;
- The approximate date and time;
- The screen or service where the information appeared;
- A description of what was exposed;
- A screenshot only where it can be safely provided without unnecessary personal information.

The Company will assess whether the incident constitutes a security or privacy event and take appropriate containment and notification steps.

---

### 15. Are there different privacy rules for business or enterprise accounts?

Yes.

For an enterprise account, the organization may control certain employee or user data and may act as the relevant data controller or business for particular processing activities.

Your enterprise agreement may determine:

- Who controls the data;
- Who can request deletion;
- How user data is exported;
- Which administrator may submit requests;
- Which retention rules apply.

As a result, some requests may need to be submitted through your organization's administrator rather than directly to the Company.
