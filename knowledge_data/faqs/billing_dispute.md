# Billing Dispute FAQ

This FAQ explains how Company handles disputed charges, billing errors, duplicate transactions, cancellation-related charges, and unrecognized payments.

### Basic Eligibility

#### 1. "I was charged the wrong amount. How long do I have to dispute it?"

For a standard incorrect billing amount, submit the dispute within **30 calendar days** of the invoice or charge date. Include your account email or customer ID, invoice or transaction number, amount, currency, and a description of what appears incorrect.

Company normally acknowledges a submitted dispute within **1 business day** and completes a standard investigation within **3–5 business days** once the required information is available.

#### 2. "I don't recognize this charge. Is it too late to report it?"

For an **unrecognized or suspected unauthorized charge**, the standard Company dispute window is **60 calendar days from the transaction date**.

Provide the transaction date, amount, currency, and any transaction identifier available. Company may require additional identity or account-security verification.

> **Do not send your full card number, CVV, password, or one-time authentication code.** Company does not need those credentials to investigate the charge.

#### 3. "I got charged twice for the same thing. Can you fix that?"

Yes. Submit a duplicate-charge dispute within **30 calendar days**.

Before treating two charges as duplicates, Company checks whether one is actually an authorization hold, a separate invoice, a recurring renewal, or a transaction in another currency.

If both are confirmed as settled charges for the same underlying transaction, Company may issue a full correction or refund for the duplicate amount.

### Cancellations & Subscription Charges

#### 4. "I canceled my subscription, so why did I get billed?"

Cancellation does not automatically invalidate a charge.

A charge may still be valid when the billing or renewal timestamp occurred **before the cancellation became effective**, or when the subscription terms require payment for the current billing period.

For a cancellation-related billing dispute, submit your claim within **30 calendar days of the charge** and include your cancellation confirmation or cancellation timestamp when available.

#### 5. "I canceled today and still got charged. What should I do ASAP?"

Submit the billing dispute immediately and provide:

1. The charge date and amount.
2. The cancellation date and time, if known.
3. The account email or customer ID.
4. The cancellation confirmation.
5. The invoice or transaction ID.

Company will compare the **billing timestamp** with the **effective cancellation timestamp**. A charge created before effective cancellation may remain valid under the subscription terms.

### Troubleshooting & Exceptions

#### 6. "My discount or promo code didn't work. Can I get the difference back?"

Usually, yes for a valid promotional billing error, but eligibility matters.

Submit the dispute within **30 calendar days** of the invoice or charge. Company checks whether:

- The promotion was still active;
- Your account qualified;
- The code was required and correctly applied;
- The promotion was limited to new or existing customers;
- Multiple promotions were improperly combined.

Promotional credits are generally not converted to cash unless the specific offer permits it.

#### 7. "The invoice has a tax I don't think I should have to pay. Can you remove it?"

Company can investigate a tax dispute, but tax charges cannot be removed solely because they appear unexpected.

The review may require your billing address, tax jurisdiction, valid exemption documentation, and other information establishing why the tax should not have applied.

A tax correction may be limited by whether the relevant tax was already remitted and whether local law permits adjustment.

#### 8. "I used a foreign card and the refund amount looks different. Did you refund me incorrectly?"

Not necessarily.

Company normally refunds the original transaction amount to the original payment method. Your bank or card issuer may convert that amount using an exchange rate that differs from the rate used when the original purchase appeared on your statement.

International refunds typically take **5–15 business days** to post, depending on the payment provider and financial institution.

### Processing Timelines

#### 9. "I submitted a dispute. How long until I hear something?"

Company normally:

- Acknowledges the dispute within **1 business day**.
- Completes a standard investigation within **3–5 business days**.
- Takes up to **10 business days** for complex cases.
- Takes approximately **5–10 business days** for fraud/security reviews.

After an approved refund is initiated, Company typically submits it within **2 business days**. Your bank or card issuer may then require another **5–10 business days** to post it.

#### 10. "My refund was approved, but I don't see the money yet. What's going on?"

Approval and bank posting are separate steps.

Once approved, Company normally initiates the refund within **2 business days**. Standard card or bank posting generally takes another **5–10 business days**. International transactions may take **5–15 business days**.

If that period has elapsed, contact support with your dispute case number and refund confirmation so the payment status can be checked.

### Escalations & Special Cases

#### 11. "You rejected my dispute and I think the decision is wrong. Can I appeal?"

Yes. Request a **secondary review** and provide the original case number.

A secondary review is most useful when you have:

- New evidence;
- A document that was not previously considered;
- A calculation showing why the amount is incorrect;
- A relevant contractual or policy provision that appears to have been overlooked.

Secondary reviews generally target **3–7 business days**.

#### 12. "I already filed a chargeback with my bank. Can Company refund me too?"

Company will not provide a duplicate recovery for the same transaction.

If a card issuer or payment provider has already reversed the transaction through a chargeback, Company will reconcile the account rather than issue a second refund for the same amount.

Where practical, customers are encouraged to contact Company first because a billing investigation may resolve an ordinary billing error without requiring a payment-provider dispute.
