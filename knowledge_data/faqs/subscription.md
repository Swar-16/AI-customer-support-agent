# Subscription FAQ

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Applies To:** Company subscriptions  
**Audience:** Customers and frontline support teams

---

## Basic Eligibility & Subscription Rules

### 1. How do I start a Company subscription?

Sign in to your Company account, choose a subscription plan, review the price and billing terms, add a valid payment method, and confirm the purchase.

After successful payment authorization, your subscription normally becomes active within **5 minutes**. If the transaction is selected for fraud or payment review, activation can take up to **24 hours**.

Keep your confirmation email or invoice as proof of purchase.

### 2. When exactly will I be charged again?

Your subscription normally renews on the same calendar day as the original billing date.

For monthly subscriptions, if that date does not exist in a particular month, the renewal occurs on the **last calendar day of that month**.

For annual subscriptions, renewal normally occurs on the same month and day each year, subject to calendar-date limitations.

Your account's subscription page and most recent billing record should be treated as the authoritative source for your next renewal date.

### 3. I forgot to cancel and just got charged. Can I get my money back?

You should contact Support **within 24 hours of the renewal charge**.

Company may review the transaction under its **24-hour billing grace period**, particularly where there is evidence that the renewal was unintended and the renewed service has not been materially used.

However, the 24-hour grace period is **not an automatic refund guarantee**. Refund eligibility can depend on the plan, usage, payment provider, contractual terms, and applicable law.

## Cancellation & Plan Changes

### 4. How do I cancel my subscription right now?

Go to:

**Account → Subscription → Manage Subscription → Cancel Subscription**

Then confirm the cancellation and verify that the account shows **Canceled** or **Scheduled for Cancellation**.

A normal cancellation stops future recurring charges while allowing you to continue using the subscription through the **already-paid billing period**, unless the applicable plan or contract states otherwise.

> **Important:** Simply deleting the app, deleting your Company account, or removing your card does **not necessarily cancel recurring billing**.

### 5. Can I cancel during my free trial without getting charged?

Yes, provided you cancel **before the trial expiration date and time**.

For example, if the promotion gives you a 14-calendar-day trial, cancellation must be completed before the displayed trial end time on day 14.

Do not rely on uninstalling the application or becoming inactive. Complete the formal cancellation process and retain the confirmation.

### 6. What happens if I downgrade my plan?

A downgrade normally takes effect at your **next renewal date**.

You should review the new plan's limits before confirming, because features, seats, storage, usage allowances, or other entitlements may decrease.

Unused prepaid subscription time is generally **not refunded as cash** merely because you downgraded.

### 7. If I upgrade, do I have to pay for the whole new month again?

Not necessarily.

Qualifying standard-plan upgrades may receive **prorated billing** for the unused portion of the existing billing period.

For example, moving from a $30/month plan to a $50/month plan with roughly half the billing period remaining could result in an incremental charge of approximately **$10**, before taxes and subject to the billing system's actual calculation.

The amount shown at confirmation is the amount you should rely on.

## Payments, Refunds & Billing Problems

### 8. My card was charged twice. What should I do?

Contact Support within **30 calendar days** and provide the account email plus the relevant transaction or invoice references.

Company will compare the billing records and payment processor information.

Confirmed duplicate charges are generally refunded to the original payment method. A typical investigation takes **1–3 business days**, with most completed cases resolved within **5–7 business days**.

### 9. My renewal payment failed. Will my subscription disappear immediately?

Usually, no.

Company may retry a failed recurring payment. You should check your payment method, verify that sufficient funds are available, and update the payment details if necessary.

Depending on the payment provider and account status, access may remain temporarily available during the payment-recovery process.

If the payment remains unresolved, the subscription may eventually be **suspended or cancelled**.

### 10. I was charged in another currency and the amount on my bank statement is different. Why?

The amount displayed at Company checkout is the applicable Company subscription price for the selected billing currency.

Your bank or payment provider may apply:

- foreign-exchange conversion;
- exchange-rate spreads;
- international transaction fees; or
- other cross-border charges.

These additional bank or card-provider charges are generally outside Company's control.

## Troubleshooting & Exceptions

### 11. I cancelled, but I was still charged. What happened?

First, check the cancellation confirmation and compare its timestamp with the renewal timestamp.

If the renewal charge occurred **before cancellation was successfully completed**, the charge may be valid even if you intended to cancel earlier.

If you cancelled before the renewal and were still charged, contact Support and provide:

- your account email;
- cancellation confirmation;
- cancellation date and time;
- charge date and time;
- invoice or transaction reference.

Billing Operations generally investigates these cases within **5–7 business days**.

### 12. I don't recognize a subscription charge. Was my account hacked?

Treat an unrecognized charge seriously.

Contact Support promptly and provide the:

- transaction date;
- charged amount and currency;
- invoice or receipt;
- account email;
- last four digits of the payment method, where applicable.

Company may temporarily restrict the account or payment method while investigating.

Do **not** send your full card number, CVV, password, or authentication codes to Support.

If your payment provider requires a formal fraud report or card replacement, you should follow its security procedures as well.

## Processing Timelines

### 13. How long does a refund take?

After Company approves a refund:

- refund initiation generally occurs within **1–3 business days**;
- card refunds typically appear within **5–10 business days**;
- bank-transfer refunds may take approximately **1–5 business days**, depending on the bank and country.

The final posting time is controlled partly by the payment processor and financial institution.

### 14. How long does it take for a subscription cancellation to work?

The cancellation itself is normally recorded **immediately after successful confirmation**.

Your paid access generally remains active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

The important distinction is:

**Cancellation date:** when future renewal is stopped.  
**Access end date:** when the already-paid subscription period expires.

A cancellation does not necessarily create an immediate refund.

## Enterprise & Special Cases

### 15. Are Enterprise subscriptions governed by the same cancellation and refund rules?

Not necessarily.

Enterprise subscriptions are governed primarily by the **signed order form, master services agreement, SLA, and other applicable contract documents**.

Those documents may specify:

- custom pricing;
- annual commitments;
- notice periods;
- payment terms such as **Net 15 or Net 30**;
- minimum seats;
- refund limitations;
- service-level commitments;
- special termination rights.

Where an Enterprise contract conflicts with a standard self-service rule, the **signed Enterprise agreement controls**, subject to applicable law.

### 16. Can I transfer my subscription to another person or company?

A subscription is generally associated with the purchasing customer or organization and may not be transferable unless the applicable plan or written Company agreement allows it.

Enterprise customers should check their contract for assignment, transfer, ownership, and change-of-control provisions.

Do not sell, sublicense, or transfer subscription access in a way that violates Company's Terms of Service.

### 17. Can I get a refund for unused time after cancelling?

Generally, **not automatically**.

For standard subscriptions, cancellation typically prevents the next renewal while the existing paid period continues to its scheduled end.

Unused time may be refundable only where:

- a specific refund right applies;
- there was duplicate or erroneous billing;
- Company materially failed to provide the purchased service;
- an unauthorized transaction is substantiated; or
- applicable law requires a refund.

## Key Rules at a Glance

| Situation | Standard Rule |
|---|---|
| Activation | Usually within **5 minutes** |
| Manual payment/fraud review | Up to **24 hours** |
| Billing grace review | Contact Support within **24 hours of renewal** |
| Duplicate-charge report | Within **30 calendar days** |
| Duplicate-charge resolution | Usually **5–7 business days** |
| Standard cancellation | Stops future renewals |
| Access after cancellation | Generally continues through paid term |
| Upgrade | May be prorated |
| Downgrade | Usually effective next renewal |
| Card refund posting | Usually **5–10 business days** |
| Enterprise terms | Signed contract controls |
| Uninstalling the app | **Does not necessarily cancel** |
| Deleting account | **Does not necessarily cancel external billing** |

> **Remember:** The safest way to confirm a subscription is cancelled is to complete the cancellation workflow, verify the account status, and retain the cancellation confirmation.
