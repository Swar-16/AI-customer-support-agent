# REFUND FAQ

## Basic Eligibility

### 1. Can I get a refund if I just changed my mind?

Usually, yes, when the purchase is eligible and you submit the request **within 14 calendar days** of the applicable purchase or delivery date.

Some products and services are **non-refundable**, including certain personalized products, consumed digital content, used services, final-sale items, and certain third-party fees.

For physical products, you may also need to return the item under an approved **RMA (Return Authorization)**.

### 2. How long do I have to ask for a refund?

The standard refund window is **14 calendar days**.

The exact starting point depends on the transaction type:

| Purchase Type | Standard Window |
|---|---:|
| Physical product | **14 calendar days from delivery** |
| Eligible digital service | **14 calendar days from purchase** |
| Subscription | Before the next renewal; initial-purchase rights may also apply |
| Duplicate or incorrect charge | Standard support review up to **60 calendar days** from the transaction date |

A request submitted after the applicable deadline is normally declined unless a documented exception applies.

### 3. What information do you need from me?

Please provide your:

- Order, invoice, subscription, or transaction ID.
- Account email or account identifier.
- Purchase date, if available.
- Reason for the refund.
- Product return and tracking information, when applicable.

For damaged products, Company may request photographs or other evidence.

Missing information can add **up to 3 business days** to the review process.

## Returns, Products & RMA

### 4. Do I need an RMA number to return a product?

Yes, an eligible physical-product return generally requires a **Return Authorization (RMA) number** before shipment.

After your refund request is approved, Company normally issues the RMA within **1 business day**.

Do not send a return to an address other than the one provided in your return instructions.

Keep your shipping receipt and tracking number until the refund is complete.

### 5. How quickly will I get my refund after you receive my returned item?

After the returned product reaches Company's designated warehouse, the standard inspection target is **2 business days**.

Once the return is approved:

1. The refund is authorized.
2. Finance or the billing system submits the refund, normally within **1–2 business days**.
3. Your bank or payment processor posts the funds.

For cards and debit payments, the full process typically takes **5–7 business days after refund initiation**.

### 6. Can I get a refund for a damaged, defective, or incorrect item?

Potentially, yes.

A damaged, materially defective, or incorrectly supplied product may qualify for a refund even when normal customer-preference return rules would not apply.

Company may request photographs or other evidence. If the return is approved because of Company error, defect, or damage covered by this policy, Company generally pays or reimburses eligible return shipping according to the approved resolution.

## Troubleshooting & Exceptions

### 7. What items cannot be refunded?

Some transactions are **non-refundable** unless required by law, contract, or an approved exception.

Examples include:

- Personalized or made-to-order products after production starts.
- Downloadable or consumable digital content after material consumption.
- Services that have already been consumed.
- Final-sale items identified as **non-refundable**.
- Certain gift-card or stored-value transactions after redemption.
- Certain third-party fees.
- Products damaged or materially altered by the customer.

The purchase-specific terms shown at the time of sale may contain additional exclusions.

### 8. I missed the 14-day deadline. Can you still refund me?

Possibly, but it is not automatic.

Late refunds are generally declined. An exception may be considered when there was a documented Company billing error, a technical failure prevented timely cancellation, a contractual/legal right applies, or an authorized service-recovery exception is appropriate.

Late exceptions require documented approval, including the reason the normal rule is being waived.

### 9. I was charged twice. What should I do?

Contact Customer Support as soon as possible and provide both transaction references if available.

Verified duplicate charges are generally eligible for correction, with standard support review available for requests submitted within **60 calendar days** of the transaction.

Company may first verify that the two charges are genuinely separate transactions rather than a temporary authorization, installment, subscription renewal, or other legitimate payment event.

### 10. I don't recognize a charge. Can you refund it?

An unrecognized charge is handled as a potential **unauthorized transaction**.

Company may ask you to complete identity or transaction verification before releasing a refund. The Fraud or Payment Risk team may investigate the transaction and can temporarily restrict refund execution while the investigation is active.

> **Important:** Do not intentionally pursue duplicate reimbursement through both Company and your bank/card issuer for the same amount. If a formal chargeback is already open, Company may suspend a duplicate refund while the dispute is resolved.

## Processing Timelines & Money

### 11. My refund was approved. Why haven't I received the money yet?

An approved refund still has to be submitted and posted by the financial institution.

Typical timing is:

| Step | Expected Time |
|---|---:|
| Eligibility review | **1–3 business days** |
| Refund initiation after approval | **1–2 business days** |
| Card/debit posting after initiation | **5–7 business days** |
| Bank transfer posting after initiation | **3–7 business days** |
| International payment posting | **5–10 business days** |

Weekends and bank holidays may affect the actual posting date.

If the applicable timeframe has passed, contact Support with your refund transaction/reference ID so Finance Operations can trace the payment.

### 12. Why is the amount in my bank account different from what I originally paid?

For international transactions, Company normally refunds the amount in the **original transaction currency**.

For example, if the original transaction was **EUR 100**, an approved full refund is normally submitted as **EUR 100**. Your bank, card issuer, or payment processor may then convert the refund into your local currency using its applicable exchange rate.

Because exchange rates can change, the local-currency amount you receive may be different from the amount originally charged.

Company generally does not reimburse foreign-exchange losses caused by currency conversion.

### 13. Do you charge a refund or restocking fee?

The standard refund processing fee is **$0**, and the standard restocking fee is **0%**.

An authorized exception may allow a restocking fee of up to **15%**, but it must be permitted by the applicable terms and properly approved.

Company will not deduct an undisclosed refund fee.

### 14. Can I get a refund for only part of my order?

Yes, when only part of the order qualifies.

Partial refunds may apply to:

- One item in a multi-item order.
- A defective component.
- Partially delivered services.
- Promotional orders where the discount must be recalculated.

For example, if a $120 order received a $20 promotion and you paid $100, the refund for a returned item may be recalculated after the promotional discount is reapplied. You receive a refund based on the **amount actually paid for the eligible item**, not necessarily its original list price.

## Subscriptions & Enterprise Accounts

### 15. I forgot to cancel my subscription before renewal. Can I get my money back?

A renewal charge is generally not refundable simply because the service was not used after renewal.

An exception may be considered where there was a documented billing-system error, you contacted Company promptly after the charge, or a contractual or legally required cancellation right applies.

To avoid future charges, cancel the subscription **before the next renewal date**.

### 16. Does this policy apply to enterprise customers?

Enterprise customers may have different refund and credit terms.

For enterprise transactions, the **executed contract, order form, statement of work, or other controlling agreement takes precedence** over this standard Refund Policy where the terms differ.

An enterprise resolution may be provided as a cash refund, credit memo, service credit, or another contractual remedy.

Enterprise refunds may require Account Management, Finance, Procurement, or Legal approval.

## Escalations

### 17. What can I do if my refund request was denied?

You may request an escalation when you believe:

- The wrong eligibility rule was applied.
- A Company billing error occurred.
- You have documentation supporting an exception.
- The refund calculation is incorrect.
- The approved refund has not been received within the stated timeframe.

Provide your case ID, transaction ID, refund amount, relevant dates, supporting documents, and any previous decision.

The normal escalation path is **Customer Support → Support Team Lead → Finance Operations**, with Fraud/Payment Risk or Legal/Compliance involved when appropriate.
