**Policy Domain:** SHIPPING  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

## Basic Shipping Eligibility

### 1. When will my order actually ship?

Most eligible orders are processed within **1–2 business days** after payment is authorized.

Orders placed after **2:00 PM local warehouse time** may be treated as received on the next business day.

Once the package is handed to the carrier, it is considered **dispatched** and transit time begins.

Typical delivery timelines are:

| Shipping Method | Typical Total Time |
|---|---:|
| Standard | 4–9 business days |
| Expedited | 2–5 business days |
| International Standard | 9–18 business days |

These are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates unless your order specifically includes a guaranteed service.

### 2. How much is shipping?

Unless a promotional or product-specific rate applies, standard domestic shipping is generally **$6.99**.

Expedited domestic shipping is generally **$19.99**.

Orders of **$75 or more** may qualify for free standard shipping.

The exact charge shown at checkout controls the transaction.

### 3. Can I change my shipping address after ordering?

Possibly, but you need to contact Customer Support **immediately**.

Address changes are generally possible only **before the package is handed to the carrier**. Once the shipment has been dispatched, the Company cannot guarantee that the carrier can reroute it.

You should provide your order number, original address, corrected address, and any verification information requested by Support.

## Tracking & Delivery Problems

### 4. I got a tracking number, but nothing is showing. What's going on?

Carrier tracking can take up to **24 hours** after dispatch to become active.

If there is still no meaningful tracking update after **48 consecutive hours**, contact Customer Support with your order and tracking numbers.

The Company can then determine whether a carrier investigation is appropriate.

### 5. My package is late. What should I do?

First, check the latest carrier tracking information.

If the package has missed its estimated delivery date, allow **1 additional business day** for normal carrier variance.

If there is still no delivery, contact Support with:

1. Order number.
2. Tracking number.
3. Delivery address.
4. Expected delivery date.
5. Any carrier exception shown in tracking.

The Company may open a carrier investigation when appropriate.

### 6. The tracking says "delivered," but I don't have my package!

Don't panic. First check:

- Your normal delivery location.
- Nearby safe locations.
- With household members.
- With building reception or management.
- With other authorized recipients.
- Any delivery photograph or signature information provided by the carrier.

If you still cannot locate it, contact Customer Support.

For certain claims, the Company may request a **written non-receipt statement** or additional verification before approving a resolution.

> **Important:** A carrier's "Delivered" scan does not automatically mean that every claim will be approved.

## Damage, Loss & Returns

### 7. My package arrived damaged. How quickly do I need to report it?

Report shipping damage within **48 hours of delivery**.

Keep the original packaging until the claim is resolved and provide:

- Order number.
- Tracking number.
- Photos of the outer packaging.
- Photo of the shipping label.
- Photos of the damaged product.
- Description of the damage.

Keeping the packaging is important because the carrier may require it during an investigation.

### 8. What happens if my package is lost?

A shipment may be treated as potentially lost when tracking has shown no meaningful movement for **7 consecutive calendar days**, or when the carrier officially reports it lost.

Contact Support with your order number and tracking number.

The Company may ask you to confirm your address and provide a written statement that the package was not received.

Carrier investigations typically take **5–10 business days**, although complex cases can take longer.

### 9. What if I entered the wrong address?

You should contact Support immediately.

Before dispatch, the Company may be able to correct the address.

After dispatch, rerouting depends on the carrier and may result in additional charges.

If the package is returned because the address was incorrect or incomplete:

- The original shipping fee may be **non-refundable**.
- Carrier return or correction fees may apply.
- Reshipping may require an additional shipping payment.

## International & Special Shipping

### 10. Do I have to pay customs or import taxes for an international order?

Usually, yes.

Unless checkout or your applicable agreement expressly says otherwise, the customer is responsible for:

- Import duties.
- Customs taxes.
- Brokerage fees.
- Destination-country charges.
- Required import documentation.

International delivery can also take longer because of customs inspection or government processing.

The normal international estimate is **9–18 business days**, but customs delays can extend that period.

### 11. Why was my order split into two packages?

The Company may split an order when products are stored at different fulfillment centers, become available at different times, require different handling, or must use different carriers.

You may receive multiple tracking numbers.

Normally, you will **not pay an additional shipping fee merely because the Company chose to split the shipment**, unless your checkout terms specifically state otherwise.

### 12. Can bad weather or carrier problems make my delivery late?

Yes.

Delivery estimates can be affected by severe weather, natural disasters, carrier strikes or outages, customs closures, government restrictions, and other events outside the Company's reasonable control.

These events may extend the delivery estimate and do not automatically constitute a Company shipping failure.

For an active delay, check tracking first. If the shipment remains inactive for **48 hours**, contact Support for assistance.
