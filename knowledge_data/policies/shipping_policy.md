# SHIPPING POLICY

**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Policy Owner:** Operations & Customer Experience  
**Policy Domain:** SHIPPING  
**Scope:** Standard, expedited, domestic, and eligible international shipments placed through the Company’s authorized sales channels.

---

## 1. General Overview & Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy establishes the rules governing shipment processing, dispatch, delivery estimates, shipping charges, delivery exceptions, address changes, lost or damaged shipments, and international shipping.

The shipping policy applies to orders accepted and fulfilled by the Company unless a written enterprise agreement or product-specific policy states otherwise.

### 1.2 Shipment Eligibility

An order is eligible for shipment when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. Payment has been successfully authorized or captured.
2. The order has passed applicable fraud and risk screening.
3. The shipping address is complete and serviceable.
4. The ordered products are available for fulfillment.
5. Any required customer verification has been completed.
6. The destination is within an area currently supported by the Company or its designated carriers.

Orders subject to fraud review, payment verification, inventory allocation, or compliance screening are **not considered dispatched** until those checks are completed.

### 1.3 Standard Processing Timeline

Orders are normally processed within **1–2 business days** after payment authorization.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding Company-observed holidays.

Orders placed after **2:00 PM local warehouse time** may be treated as received on the next business day for processing purposes.

### 1.4 Shipping Service Levels

| Service | Processing Time | Carrier Transit Estimate | Typical Total Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Standard | 1–2 business days | 3–7 business days | 4–9 business days |
| Expedited | 1–2 business days | 1–3 business days | 2–5 business days |
| International Standard | 2–3 business days | 7–15 business days | 9–18 business days |

Transit estimates begin **after dispatch**, not when the order is placed.

> **Important:** A shipping estimate is not a guaranteed delivery date unless the applicable order confirmation expressly identifies the service as guaranteed.

### 1.5 Shipment Confirmation

When an order is dispatched, the Company will normally provide:

- Carrier name.
- Tracking number.
- Shipment date.
- Shipping service.
- Destination postal code.
- Estimated delivery date, where available.

Tracking information may require up to **24 hours** after carrier handoff to display movement.

## 2. Step-by-Step Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Customer Order and Shipment Process

1. **Place the order**
   - Customer submits the order with a complete delivery address.
   - Customer selects an available shipping method.

2. **Payment authorization**
   - Payment must be successfully authorized.
   - Orders with failed or pending payment are not released for shipment.

3. **Order validation**
   - The Company validates inventory, address information, fraud controls, and applicable shipping restrictions.

4. **Fulfillment**
   - Warehouse personnel pick and pack eligible items.
   - Orders may be split into multiple shipments when products originate from different fulfillment locations.

5. **Carrier handoff**
   - The completed package is transferred to the selected or assigned carrier.
   - The shipment status changes to **Dispatched**.

6. **Tracking activation**
   - A tracking number is issued.
   - Tracking may take up to **24 hours** to become active.

7. **Delivery**
   - The carrier attempts delivery according to its operating procedures.
   - Delivery estimates may change because of weather, carrier disruptions, customs, incorrect addresses, or other events outside Company control.

### 2.2 Requesting an Address Change Before Dispatch

Customers should request an address correction immediately after discovering an error.

The customer must:

1. Contact Customer Support through an authorized support channel.
2. Provide the order number.
3. Confirm the original shipping address.
4. Provide the corrected address.
5. Complete any identity or order verification requested by the agent.

Address changes are generally permitted **only before carrier handoff**.

The Company does not guarantee that a requested change can be completed before dispatch.

### 2.3 Tracking a Shipment

Customers should:

1. Open the shipment confirmation.
2. Locate the tracking number.
3. Open the carrier tracking page or the Company's order-tracking interface.
4. Review the latest scan and estimated delivery date.

If tracking has not updated for **48 consecutive hours** after dispatch, the customer may contact Customer Support for investigation.

### 2.4 Shipment Delayed Beyond the Estimated Delivery Date

If a shipment has not arrived by the estimated delivery date:

1. Allow at least **1 additional business day** for normal carrier variance.
2. Review the tracking history for attempted delivery, address issues, customs holds, or exception scans.
3. If no delivery occurs and the shipment remains inactive, contact Customer Support.
4. Provide the order number and tracking number.
5. The Company may open a carrier investigation.

### 2.5 Reporting a Lost Shipment

A shipment may be treated as potentially lost when:

- Tracking has had no meaningful movement for **7 consecutive calendar days**, or
- The carrier officially reports the shipment as lost, or
- The carrier marks the package as delivered but the customer cannot locate it after completing the required delivery-location checks.

The customer should contact Support with:

- Order number.
- Tracking number.
- Delivery address.
- Date the shipment was expected.
- Confirmation that the package was not received.

The Company may request a written statement of non-receipt, particularly for high-value orders or fraud-risk cases.

### 2.6 Reporting Damage

Visible shipping damage should be reported within **48 hours of delivery**.

The customer should provide:

1. Order number.
2. Tracking number.
3. Photographs of the external packaging.
4. Photographs of the shipping label.
5. Photographs showing the damaged product.
6. A description of the damage.

Customers should retain the packaging until the claim is resolved.

## 3. Exceptions, Restrictions & Special Cases

### 3.1 Incorrect or Incomplete Address

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate shipping address.

If an order cannot be delivered because the customer supplied an incorrect, incomplete, or invalid address:

- Additional shipping charges may apply.
- The Company may require payment of carrier redelivery or rerouting fees.
- The original shipping fee may be **non-refundable**.
- A returned package may require a new shipping charge before reshipment.

### 3.2 Undeliverable or Refused Packages

If a carrier returns a package because the recipient refused delivery or the address was undeliverable:

1. The Company may wait for carrier confirmation of return.
2. Any applicable refund will be processed under the Company's refund rules.
3. Original shipping charges may be deducted when the failed delivery resulted from customer-provided information or customer refusal.
4. Reshipment requires confirmation of the correct address and may require additional shipping payment.

### 3.3 Split Shipments

An order may be split when:

- Items are stocked at different facilities.
- Products have different handling requirements.
- Certain items require specialized carriers.
- Inventory becomes available at different times.

Customers are generally **not charged additional shipping fees solely because the Company elects to split an otherwise single shipment**, unless the order confirmation expressly states otherwise.

Each package may have a separate tracking number.

### 3.4 Backordered Products

If an item is temporarily unavailable:

- The Company may hold the order until inventory is available.
- The Company may ship available items separately.
- The customer may be contacted if the expected delay exceeds **7 business days**.
- Cancellation options may depend on the product and applicable cancellation policy.

### 3.5 Fraud and Security Review

For fraud-prevention purposes, the Company may delay shipment while verifying:

- Billing and shipping information.
- Payment ownership.
- Order value.
- Unusual ordering patterns.
- High-risk destination or delivery characteristics.

Security reviews generally target completion within **24 hours**, but complex cases may take longer.

Customer Support may not disclose internal fraud-detection rules or risk thresholds.

### 3.6 PO Boxes and Restricted Addresses

Some carriers or products may not support:

- PO Boxes.
- Military addresses.
- Freight-forwarding addresses.
- Parcel lockers.
- Remote or restricted delivery locations.

If the selected shipping service cannot deliver to the provided address, the Company may request an alternative address or shipping method.

### 3.7 International Shipping

International shipments may be subject to:

- Import duties.
- Customs taxes.
- Brokerage fees.
- Import permits.
- Product restrictions.
- Customs inspection.
- Destination-country regulations.

Unless expressly stated otherwise at checkout, **customs duties, import taxes, and destination-country fees are the customer's responsibility**.

Customs delays are outside the Company's standard transit-time commitment.

### 3.8 Products With Shipping Restrictions

Certain products may require special handling or may be unavailable for shipment to particular destinations.

Restrictions may apply to:

- Hazardous materials.
- Batteries.
- Temperature-sensitive products.
- Oversized products.
- Regulated products.
- Products restricted by destination-country law.

The Company may cancel or modify shipment arrangements when legal or carrier restrictions prevent fulfillment.

### 3.9 Weather, Natural Disasters, and Carrier Disruptions

Delivery estimates may be extended by events outside the Company's reasonable control, including:

- Severe weather.
- Flooding.
- Earthquakes.
- Strikes.
- Civil emergencies.
- Carrier network outages.
- Customs closures.
- Government restrictions.

Such events do not automatically constitute a Company shipping failure.

### 3.10 Enterprise and Contract Customers

Customers operating under a negotiated enterprise agreement may receive different:

- Shipping rates.
- Carrier arrangements.
- Delivery commitments.
- Service-level agreements.
- Claims procedures.

Where an executed enterprise agreement conflicts with this policy, the **executed agreement controls** for the affected transaction.

## 4. Processing Times, Fees & Financial Terms

### 4.1 Shipping Fees

Shipping charges are calculated based on applicable factors such as:

- Destination.
- Package dimensions and weight.
- Selected shipping service.
- Number of packages.
- Product-specific handling requirements.
- Applicable carrier surcharges.

The applicable shipping fee is displayed during checkout before order submission.

### 4.2 Illustrative Standard Shipping Rates

Unless a product-specific or promotional rate applies:

| Service | Domestic Fee |
|---|---:|
| Standard | $6.99 |
| Expedited | $19.99 |
| Free Standard Shipping | Orders of $75 or more |

Currency is **USD** unless otherwise stated at checkout.

Promotional shipping rates may have separate eligibility requirements and expiration dates.

### 4.3 Shipping Fee Refunds

Shipping charges are generally refundable only when:

- The Company shipped the wrong item.
- The Company caused the shipment failure.
- The order was cancelled before dispatch and the applicable policy permits a refund.
- A guaranteed delivery commitment was expressly breached and the applicable guarantee provides a refund.

Shipping charges may be **non-refundable** for customer-caused delivery failures, refused packages, incorrect addresses, or customs refusal.

### 4.4 Carrier Surcharges

Additional carrier charges may apply to:

- Address correction.
- Remote-area delivery.
- Oversized packages.
- Residential delivery.
- Rerouting.
- Redelivery.
- Customs or brokerage processing.

Where the charge results from customer-requested changes after dispatch, the customer may be responsible for the additional amount.

### 4.5 International Currency Handling

International shipping charges may be displayed in the customer's selected checkout currency.

The final amount charged may differ from an estimated local-currency amount because of:

- Exchange-rate movements.
- Payment-provider conversion rates.
- Bank fees.
- International transaction fees.

The Company does not control fees independently imposed by the customer's bank or payment provider.

### 4.6 Refund Timing Following Shipping Claims

When a refund is approved:

| Refund Stage | Target Time |
|---|---:|
| Claim decision | Typically within 3–5 business days after required documentation is complete |
| Company refund initiation | Within 2 business days after approval |
| Card/bank posting | Typically 5–10 business days |

The actual posting period depends on the financial institution.

## 5. Escalations & Customer Responsibilities

### 5.1 Customer Responsibilities

Customers must:

- Provide an accurate and complete delivery address.
- Select an appropriate shipping method.
- Monitor tracking information.
- Be available to receive deliveries when required.
- Report damage within **48 hours**.
- Report suspected loss or non-receipt promptly.
- Retain damaged packaging when a carrier claim may be required.
- Pay applicable customs duties and destination-country charges.
- Provide requested documentation truthfully and completely.

### 5.2 Required Documentation

Depending on the case, Support may request:

- Order confirmation.
- Tracking number.
- Delivery address.
- Photographs.
- Proof of delivery.
- Written non-receipt declaration.
- Carrier claim information.
- Identity verification.
- Customs documentation.

High-value or suspicious claims may require enhanced verification.

### 5.3 Shipping Claim Escalation Levels

| Level | Trigger | Target Response |
|---|---|---:|
| Level 1 | General tracking or delivery question | Within 1 business day |
| Level 2 | Delayed, damaged, or potentially lost shipment | Within 2 business days |
| Level 3 | High-value claim, repeated carrier failure, or disputed delivery | Within 3 business days |
| Level 4 | Formal complaint or unresolved escalation | Within 5 business days |

Response time refers to the Company's target for responding to the customer, not necessarily final claim resolution.

### 5.4 Carrier Investigation

When a carrier investigation is required, the Company may submit a claim on the customer's behalf.

The customer must cooperate with requests for:

- Confirmation of non-receipt.
- Delivery-location checks.
- Photographic evidence.
- Signature verification.
- Address confirmation.

Carrier investigations may require **5–10 business days** or longer depending on the carrier and circumstances.

### 5.5 Delivered but Not Received

If tracking states **Delivered** but the customer cannot locate the package, the customer should:

1. Check the delivery location and surrounding areas.
2. Check with household members, reception desks, building management, or authorized recipients.
3. Review any carrier delivery photograph or signature information.
4. Contact Customer Support if the package remains missing.

The Company may initiate an investigation after these checks.

> **Important:** A "Delivered" scan does not automatically guarantee claim approval. Claims may be subject to carrier evidence, account history, fraud controls, and the applicable order value.

### 5.6 Disputed Shipping Charges

Customers disputing a shipping charge should contact Support within **30 calendar days** of the charge.

The request should include:

- Order number.
- Charged amount.
- Reason for dispute.
- Relevant supporting evidence.

The Company will review the transaction and communicate the outcome.

### 5.7 Policy Precedence

If another binding document expressly governs shipping for a specific transaction, the following precedence generally applies:

1. Applicable law or regulation.
2. Executed enterprise or negotiated agreement.
3. Product-specific terms expressly incorporated into the order.
4. Order confirmation.
5. This Shipping Policy.
6. General customer-support guidance.

The Company may update this policy prospectively. The version applicable to an order is generally the version in effect when the order was accepted, unless applicable law requires otherwise.
