## Section 1: General Overview & Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Refund Principles

Company issues refunds only where the transaction meets the eligibility conditions in this policy, an applicable product-specific policy, or a written contractual commitment.

A refund is a reversal of an eligible payment. A refund does not automatically include reimbursement for third-party charges, foreign-exchange losses, expedited shipping, customs charges, taxes that cannot legally be recovered, or optional service fees unless expressly stated below.

The following terms apply throughout this policy:

- **Refund Request:** A customer request for reversal of an eligible charge.
- **Return Authorization / RMA:** The authorization issued for an eligible physical-product return.
- **Business Day:** Monday through Friday, excluding Company-observed banking holidays.
- **Calendar Day:** Any day, including weekends and holidays.
- **Original Payment Method:** The payment instrument or account originally charged.
- **Refund Eligible:** A transaction satisfying all applicable timing, product, payment, and account requirements.

### 1.2 Standard Refund Eligibility

Unless a product-specific rule or executed contract states otherwise, a standard refund request is eligible when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The request is submitted **within 14 calendar days** of the applicable purchase, activation, or delivery date.
2. The customer can provide sufficient transaction information to identify the purchase.
3. The transaction is not subject to a stated **non-refundable** exclusion.
4. The account is not under active fraud, sanctions, abuse, or payment-security investigation that prevents refund processing.
5. For physical goods, the product is returned in accordance with the applicable return instructions and within the required return window.
6. The refund has not already been completed, reversed, or otherwise resolved through a prior credit, chargeback, replacement, or contractual settlement.

### 1.3 Eligible Refund Triggers

A refund may be approved for one or more of the following reasons:

| Refund Trigger | Standard Eligibility |
|---|---|
| Customer cancellation | **Within 14 calendar days**, subject to exclusions |
| Duplicate charge | Eligible after transaction verification |
| Incorrect amount charged | Eligible for the overcharged amount |
| Failed or materially incomplete service | Eligible following operational verification |
| Product not received | Eligible after delivery-status investigation |
| Damaged or materially defective product | Eligible after required evidence is reviewed |
| Unauthorized transaction | Handled under the fraud/payment-investigation process |
| Company-approved service recovery | May be approved outside standard windows by authorized personnel |
| Contractual refund right | Governed by the applicable executed agreement |

### 1.4 Refund Request Prerequisites

Customers should provide:

- Order, invoice, subscription, or transaction ID.
- Email address or account identifier associated with the transaction.
- Date of purchase, when available.
- Reason for the refund.
- For physical goods: product condition and return-shipment details.
- For damaged products: photographs or other reasonable evidence when requested.
- For unauthorized transactions: additional identity or payment-verification information requested by the fraud team.

Missing information may delay processing by **up to 3 business days** while Company requests clarification.

### 1.5 Refund Eligibility by Transaction Type

| Transaction Type | Standard Refund Window | Key Condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Physical goods | **14 calendar days** from delivery | Return must satisfy product-specific return conditions |
| Prepaid digital service not materially consumed | **14 calendar days** from purchase | Service usage may affect eligibility |
| Subscription cancellation | Before next renewal for recurring billing; **14 calendar days** for initial purchase where applicable | Previously consumed service may be non-refundable |
| Duplicate payment | **60 calendar days** from transaction date for standard support review | Duplicate transaction must be verified |
| Incorrect charge | **60 calendar days** from transaction date for standard support review | Refund generally limited to disputed amount |
| Enterprise transaction | Per executed agreement | Contract terms control where different |

## Section 2: Step-by-Step Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Customer Refund Request Procedure

Customers must complete the following process:

1. **Locate the transaction**
   - Find the order number, invoice number, subscription ID, or transaction ID.
   - Confirm the email or account associated with the purchase.

2. **Submit the refund request**
   - Submit the request through the Company's designated customer-support or account channel.
   - State the reason for the refund and provide supporting information.

3. **Verify eligibility**
   - Company checks the purchase date, refund window, payment status, product classification, usage status, and applicable exclusions.

4. **Receive a decision**
   - Company will approve, partially approve, or decline the request.
   - When additional information is required, the customer receives a request for documentation.

5. **Obtain an RMA when physical goods are involved**
   - Eligible physical-product returns require an **RMA authorization number** before shipment unless Customer Support expressly waives the requirement.
   - The customer must use the return instructions associated with the RMA.

6. **Return the product**
   - Package the product securely.
   - Include the RMA information exactly as instructed.
   - Keep the carrier receipt and tracking number until the refund is complete.

7. **Company verifies the return**
   - Warehouse or Returns Operations confirms receipt and, where relevant, product condition.
   - Standard inspection target is **2 business days** after warehouse receipt.

8. **Refund is authorized**
   - Finance or the authorized billing system initiates the refund to the **original payment method** unless an approved exception applies.

9. **Refund confirmation is issued**
   - The customer receives confirmation when the refund has been submitted.
   - The confirmation amount may differ from the original transaction when partial refund, tax adjustment, or approved fee deduction applies.

### 2.2 Agent Refund Procedure

Customer Support agents must:

1. Confirm account ownership and identify the transaction.
2. Review the refund window and applicable product policy.
3. Check for previous refunds, credits, chargebacks, replacements, or duplicate requests.
4. Check fraud and payment-risk indicators.
5. Confirm whether the item or service is refundable.
6. Record the refund reason using the approved disposition code.
7. Obtain supervisor approval where required by the authorization matrix.
8. Create an RMA for an eligible physical return.
9. Record the approved refund amount and currency.
10. Initiate the refund or route the request to Finance Operations.
11. Record the refund transaction/reference ID in the case.
12. Communicate the expected posting timeframe to the customer.

### 2.3 Authorization Matrix

| Refund Amount / Situation | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| Up to **$250 USD equivalent** | Authorized Customer Support agent |
| **$250.01–$1,000 USD equivalent** | Senior Support agent or team lead |
| Above **$1,000 USD equivalent** | Finance Operations approval |
| Outside standard 14-day window | Team lead approval plus documented exception reason |
| Suspected fraud / unauthorized payment | Fraud or Payment Risk team |
| Enterprise contractual exception | Account owner plus Finance/Legal as required by contract |
| Refund involving sanctions, legal hold, or law-enforcement request | Legal/Compliance review |

Equivalent thresholds are converted using Company's approved internal exchange-rate methodology at the time of approval.

### 2.4 Partial Refund Procedure

A partial refund may be issued when:

- Only part of an order is eligible.
- A discount or promotion must be recalculated.
- A service was partially consumed.
- A defective component is being credited without return of the complete order.
- A contractual or operational remedy specifies a partial credit.

The agent must record the calculation basis and the exact amount refunded.

## Section 3: Exceptions, Restrictions & Special Cases

### 3.1 Non-Refundable Items and Services

The following are generally **non-refundable** unless required by law, contract, or an expressly approved service-recovery exception:

- Gift cards and stored-value balances after redemption or activation, except where required by applicable law.
- Customized, personalized, or made-to-order physical products after production has begun.
- Downloadable or consumable digital content after material access or consumption.
- Usage-based services already consumed.
- Third-party fees explicitly identified as non-refundable.
- Final-sale products identified as **non-refundable** at the time of purchase.
- Products altered, damaged, modified, or rendered materially incomplete by the customer, except where the defect is attributable to Company.
- Fraudulent transactions or transactions lacking required verification.

### 3.2 Late Refund Requests

Requests received after the applicable refund window are normally declined.

A late request may be escalated when one or more of the following applies:

- Customer reports a Company-caused billing error.
- The transaction was materially misrepresented or incorrectly billed.
- A technical failure prevented the customer from exercising an available cancellation mechanism.
- The delay resulted from a documented Company operational error.
- A mandatory legal or contractual obligation applies.
- Customer Support grants a documented service-recovery exception.

A late exception must include the reason, evidence considered, approving authority, and final disposition.

### 3.3 Fraud Prevention and Unauthorized Transactions

Company may delay, restrict, or withhold refund execution while investigating suspected fraud, account takeover, payment abuse, or duplicate reimbursement.

> **Critical warning:** Company will not redirect a verified refund to an unrelated third-party payment method solely because the customer requests it. Refund routing is designed to prevent payment fraud and unauthorized diversion of funds.

For disputed unauthorized transactions:

1. Company verifies the transaction and customer account.
2. Payment-risk controls may temporarily restrict refund execution.
3. The customer may be asked to complete identity or transaction verification.
4. Company investigates the transaction.
5. The outcome is recorded as refund, decline, or payment-dispute escalation.

A customer should not submit both a refund request and a payment-provider chargeback for the same unresolved amount unless instructed to do so.

### 3.4 Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If a card issuer, bank, or payment provider has already opened a formal dispute:

- Company may suspend duplicate refund processing for the disputed amount.
- Evidence may be submitted to the payment provider.
- Any refund already issued may affect the dispute outcome.
- Once a chargeback is finalized, Company will not issue a second refund for the same amount unless Finance explicitly approves an exception.

### 3.5 International Transactions

For international purchases:

- Refunds are generally sent in the **original transaction currency**.
- The final amount received in the customer's local currency may vary due to the card issuer, bank, payment processor, or exchange-rate movements.
- Company does not guarantee reimbursement of foreign-exchange losses caused after the original transaction.
- Customs duties, import taxes, brokerage charges, and similar third-party amounts are refundable only when Company actually receives or is legally able to recover those amounts.
- International return shipping is generally the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to Company error, defect, damage, or a policy-covered exception.

### 3.6 Promotional Discounts and Bundles

For discounted purchases, Company calculates the refund using the applicable allocation methodology.

If a promotional discount required multiple qualifying items, returning only part of the transaction may cause the discount to be recalculated.

Example:

- Original order: $120
- Promotional discount: $20
- Net paid: $100
- Eligible returned item: $60
- Revised discount after return: $5
- Refund: **$55**

The refund cannot exceed the amount actually paid for the eligible item after applicable discounts.

### 3.7 Subscriptions and Recurring Charges

Customers should cancel before the next renewal date to prevent the next billing cycle.

A renewal charge is generally not refundable solely because the customer did not use the service after renewal.

An exception may be considered when:

- The renewal was caused by a documented billing-system error.
- The customer contacted Company promptly after the charge.
- A contractual or legally mandated cancellation right applies.
- An authorized service-recovery exception is approved.

Where an eligible subscription refund is granted, access may be terminated immediately or at the end of the relevant billing period, depending on the approved resolution.

### 3.8 Enterprise Customers

For enterprise accounts:

- The **executed master agreement, order form, statement of work, service agreement, or other controlling contract takes precedence** over this standard policy where terms differ.
- Contractual service credits are not automatically convertible into cash refunds.
- Enterprise refunds may require procurement, accounts-payable, account-owner, or Finance approval.
- Refunds may be issued by credit memo or another contractual mechanism instead of the original payment method where commercially or contractually appropriate.

## Section 4: Processing Times, Fees & Financial Terms

### 4.1 Standard Processing Timeline

| Stage | Standard Target |
|---|---:|
| Customer request acknowledgement | Within **1 business day** |
| Initial eligibility review | **1–3 business days** |
| Additional-document review | **Up to 3 business days** after receipt |
| RMA issuance, when approved | Within **1 business day** of approval |
| Warehouse inspection after return receipt | **2 business days** |
| Refund initiation after final approval | **1–2 business days** |
| Card/debit payment posting | Typically **5–7 business days** after initiation |
| Bank transfer posting | Typically **3–7 business days** after initiation |
| International payment posting | Typically **5–10 business days** after initiation |

Payment processors and financial institutions may take longer than the Company processing target.

### 4.2 Refund Fees

Unless a product-specific policy or contract states otherwise:

| Fee Type | Standard Treatment |
|---|---|
| Standard refund processing fee | **$0** |
| Restocking fee for standard eligible return | **0%** |
| Restocking fee for authorized exception | Up to **15%**, only when disclosed and approved |
| Return shipping for defective/wrong item | Company-paid or reimbursed as approved |
| Return shipping for customer preference return | Customer-paid unless otherwise stated |
| International customs/brokerage charges | Generally non-refundable third-party charges |
| Payment-provider or bank conversion fee | Generally not reimbursed |

No undisclosed refund fee may be deducted from an approved customer refund.

### 4.3 Refund Currency and Exchange Rates

Refunds are normally issued in the currency of the original transaction.

For an original transaction of **EUR 100**, Company will generally submit a **EUR 100** refund when the full transaction is refundable. If the customer's bank subsequently converts that amount into another currency, the bank's applicable exchange rate governs the final local-currency amount.

Company does not guarantee that the customer will receive the same local-currency value as the original purchase.

### 4.4 Refund Amount Calculation

The final refund amount may include:

- Eligible product or service price.
- Recoverable transaction taxes, where applicable.
- Approved shipping reimbursement.
- Approved service-recovery amounts.

The final amount may exclude:

- Non-refundable third-party fees.
- Non-refundable customs or brokerage charges.
- Amounts attributable to customer-caused damage.
- Previously refunded amounts.
- Value already consumed where the policy permits only a partial refund.

### 4.5 Refund Confirmation and Reconciliation

Each completed refund must have:

- Original transaction ID.
- Refund transaction/reference ID.
- Refund amount.
- Currency.
- Approval authority.
- Refund date.
- Refund reason/disposition code.

Finance Operations reconciles outstanding refund transactions against the payment ledger according to standard financial-control procedures.

## Section 5: Escalations & Customer Responsibilities

### 5.1 Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

1. Submitting accurate transaction information.
2. Requesting a refund within the applicable deadline.
3. Providing requested documentation promptly.
4. Returning physical goods using the approved RMA instructions.
5. Retaining tracking and shipment evidence until the refund is completed.
6. Not intentionally seeking duplicate reimbursement through multiple channels.
7. Monitoring the original payment account for the refund posting.
8. Notifying Company if a refund remains absent after the stated processor timeframe.

### 5.2 Escalation Path

A customer may request escalation when:

- No eligibility decision has been provided within **3 business days** after all required information is received.
- An approved refund has not been initiated within the stated timeframe.
- A refund has been initiated but has not posted after the expected payment-provider timeframe.
- The customer disputes a refund calculation.
- A policy exception is supported by documented facts.
- The customer believes the transaction was unauthorized.

Standard escalation path:

1. **Customer Support Agent**
2. **Support Team Lead**
3. **Finance Operations**, for payment or calculation disputes
4. **Fraud/Payment Risk**, for unauthorized or suspicious transactions
5. **Legal/Compliance**, for contractual, regulatory, or legal disputes

### 5.3 Required Escalation Documentation

An escalation should include:

- Case/ticket ID.
- Transaction ID.
- Original payment date.
- Amount and currency.
- Refund request date.
- Prior decision and reason.
- Supporting documents.
- RMA number, if applicable.
- Tracking number, if applicable.
- Refund transaction/reference ID, if already issued.

### 5.4 Missing or Delayed Refunds

If a customer reports that an approved refund has not arrived:

1. Confirm the refund transaction/reference ID.
2. Confirm the refund amount and currency.
3. Confirm the date Company submitted the refund.
4. Compare elapsed time with the applicable **5–7 business day** standard card/debit posting window or **3–7 business day** bank-transfer window.
5. Ask the customer to verify the original payment account.
6. If outside the applicable window, Finance Operations traces the payment.
7. If the processor confirms completion, the customer may be directed to the issuing bank or payment provider using the refund reference information.

### 5.5 Policy Exceptions

Only authorized personnel may approve policy exceptions.

Every exception must document:

- The rule being waived.
- The reason for the waiver.
- Evidence reviewed.
- Financial impact.
- Approver identity/role.
- Customer communication.
- Whether the exception establishes no precedent for future claims.

### 5.6 Record Retention

Refund-related records must be retained according to Company's applicable financial, contractual, privacy, and records-retention requirements.

Operational case notes should be sufficiently detailed for another authorized employee to understand:

- What the customer requested.
- What eligibility rules were applied.
- What evidence was reviewed.
- Who approved the outcome.
- What amount was refunded.
- When the refund was initiated.

### 5.7 Final Policy Rule

Where multiple provisions appear to apply, use the following precedence:

1. Applicable law or mandatory regulatory requirement.
2. Executed customer contract.
3. Product-specific published terms.
4. This Refund Policy.
5. Internal service-recovery discretion.

No employee may promise a refund that conflicts with a higher-precedence requirement without obtaining the appropriate approval.
