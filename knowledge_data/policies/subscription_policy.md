# Subscription Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Policy Owner:** Customer Experience & Billing Operations  
**Policy Type:** Formal Legal & Operational Policy  
**Scope:** All paid subscriptions offered directly by Company through its website, supported applications, and approved billing channels  
**Applies To:** Standard, Professional, and Enterprise subscription customers unless a signed agreement states otherwise  
**Primary Currency:** USD unless the applicable checkout page, invoice, order form, or contract states a different billing currency

---

## 1. General Overview & Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Subscription Model

A **subscription** is a recurring commercial arrangement under which a customer receives continued access to designated Company products or services in exchange for a recurring fee.

Subscriptions may be:

- **Monthly**, billed every calendar month.
- **Annual**, billed once every 12 months.
- **Enterprise**, billed according to the applicable signed order form or master services agreement.

The exact subscription tier, price, billing frequency, included usage, limits, and renewal date are displayed at checkout or in the applicable contractual documentation.

### 1.2 Eligibility

A customer may purchase and maintain a subscription only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The customer is legally capable of entering into a binding agreement in the applicable jurisdiction.
2. The billing information supplied to Company is complete and accurate.
3. The selected payment method is valid and authorized for recurring charges.
4. The customer is not subject to an account suspension, termination, sanctions restriction, or fraud-prevention block.
5. The subscription is purchased through an authorized Company billing channel.

Company may reject or cancel a subscription before activation where:

- payment authorization fails;
- fraud or abuse indicators are detected;
- the customer attempts to circumvent usage, account, or geographic restrictions;
- materially incorrect billing information prevents processing; or
- applicable law prohibits the transaction.

### 1.3 Activation

Subscription access generally becomes active after successful payment authorization.

For card or wallet transactions, activation normally occurs within **5 minutes**. In exceptional cases involving manual fraud review, activation may take up to **24 hours**.

If payment succeeds but access remains unavailable for more than **24 hours**, the customer should contact Support and provide the transaction reference, account email, and relevant invoice or receipt.

### 1.4 Trial Periods

Where a promotional or free trial is expressly offered:

- The trial duration is the period displayed at sign-up, commonly **7 or 14 calendar days**.
- A trial may require a valid payment method.
- Unless the offer expressly states otherwise, the subscription automatically converts to the displayed paid plan at the end of the trial.
- Cancellation before the trial expiration prevents the next recurring charge.
- Deleting an application or becoming inactive does **not** constitute cancellation.

A promotional trial may be limited to **one trial per customer, payment method, organization, or household**, depending on the promotion.

### 1.5 Billing Date and Renewal

The **renewal date** is the date on which the subscription is scheduled to renew.

For monthly subscriptions:

- Billing generally occurs on the same calendar day each month.
- If that day does not exist in a particular month, billing occurs on the **last calendar day of that month**.

For annual subscriptions:

- Billing generally occurs on the same month and day each year.
- Where the corresponding date does not exist, renewal occurs on the final valid calendar day of that month.

A cancellation request received after a renewal charge has successfully processed normally prevents future renewals but does not automatically reverse the charge.

### 1.6 24-Hour Billing Grace Period

Company provides a **24-hour administrative grace period** for certain cancellation and payment-support situations.

The grace period:

- does **not** guarantee a refund;
- does **not** automatically apply to every plan or payment method;
- does not override a signed Enterprise agreement;
- does not apply where required action is prohibited by the payment processor or law.

Where approved by Support, a renewal charge may be reviewed for reversal if the customer contacts Company within **24 hours of the charge** and demonstrates that the subscription was not intentionally renewed.

Approval is discretionary unless a separate contractual or mandatory consumer-rights rule provides a stronger entitlement.

## 2. Step-by-Step Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Starting a Subscription

1. Sign in to the Company account associated with the subscription.
2. Select the desired subscription tier.
3. Review the displayed:
   - recurring price;
   - billing frequency;
   - applicable taxes;
   - included limits;
   - renewal terms;
   - cancellation conditions.
4. Enter a valid payment method.
5. Confirm authorization for recurring billing.
6. Submit the purchase.
7. Wait for payment confirmation.
8. Verify that the subscription status shows **Active**.
9. Retain the confirmation email or invoice for accounting and dispute purposes.

### 2.2 Upgrading a Subscription

1. Open **Account → Subscription → Change Plan**.
2. Select the higher subscription tier.
3. Review the displayed prorated amount, if applicable.
4. Confirm the plan change.
5. Complete any required payment authorization.
6. Confirm that the account displays the new tier.

Unless otherwise stated, an upgrade takes effect **immediately after successful payment authorization**.

For standard self-service subscriptions, the incremental amount may be calculated on a prorated basis for the unused portion of the current billing period.

### 2.3 Downgrading a Subscription

1. Open **Account → Subscription → Change Plan**.
2. Select the lower subscription tier.
3. Review changes to usage limits, features, seats, storage, and other entitlements.
4. Confirm the downgrade.
5. Save or acknowledge the change.

A downgrade normally takes effect at the **next renewal date** unless the checkout flow expressly states immediate effect.

Unused prepaid subscription time is generally **not converted to cash** solely because of a downgrade.

### 2.4 Cancelling a Subscription

1. Sign in to the account that owns the subscription.
2. Open **Account → Subscription → Manage Subscription**.
3. Select **Cancel Subscription**.
4. Review the displayed cancellation effective date.
5. Select a cancellation reason, where requested.
6. Confirm cancellation.
7. Verify that the subscription status shows **Canceled** or **Scheduled for Cancellation**.
8. Retain the cancellation confirmation email or confirmation number.

A normal cancellation stops future recurring billing. Unless otherwise stated, access remains available until the end of the already-paid subscription period.

### 2.5 Cancelling During a Free Trial

1. Open the subscription management page before the trial expires.
2. Select **Cancel Trial** or **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Confirm cancellation.
4. Verify the cancellation timestamp.

Cancellation must be completed **before the stated trial expiration date and time** to prevent conversion to the paid subscription.

### 2.6 Re-Subscribing After Cancellation

1. Sign in to the prior Company account.
2. Open the subscription page.
3. Select an available subscription tier.
4. Confirm pricing and billing terms.
5. Submit payment.
6. Verify activation.

A previously cancelled customer may be subject to the then-current price rather than the historical subscription price.

### 2.7 Changing Payment Method

1. Open **Account → Billing → Payment Methods**.
2. Add a valid replacement payment method.
3. Complete any verification required by the payment processor.
4. Set the replacement method as the default recurring payment method.
5. Remove the expired or unwanted method, where permitted.

Changing the payment method does not itself cancel a subscription.

### 2.8 Handling a Failed Renewal

When a recurring payment fails:

1. Company may retry the payment using the configured retry schedule.
2. The customer should verify the payment method and available funds.
3. The customer should update payment information if necessary.
4. Company may temporarily restrict paid features after the applicable grace period.
5. If the payment remains unresolved, the subscription may be suspended or cancelled.

The exact retry sequence may vary by payment processor and payment method.

## 3. Exceptions, Restrictions & Special Cases

### 3.1 Non-Refundable Subscription Charges

Unless otherwise required by law, the following charges are generally **non-refundable**:

- subscription time already consumed;
- annual subscription fees after substantial use of the annual service period;
- usage-based overage charges;
- one-time setup or implementation fees;
- Enterprise onboarding fees;
- charges expressly identified as non-refundable at checkout;
- payments forfeited because of violation of the Terms of Service.

A refund exception may be considered where:

- duplicate billing occurred;
- Company materially failed to provide the purchased service;
- an unauthorized transaction is substantiated;
- a billing system error occurred; or
- applicable consumer-protection law requires a refund.

### 3.2 Duplicate Charges

If the same subscription is charged twice for the same billing period:

1. Contact Support within **30 calendar days** of identifying the duplicate charge.
2. Provide both transaction identifiers if available.
3. Company will investigate the billing records.
4. Confirmed duplicate charges are generally refunded to the original payment method.

Duplicate-charge reviews normally complete within **5–7 business days**.

### 3.3 Unauthorized Subscription Transactions

Customers must promptly report transactions they believe were unauthorized.

Company may request:

- transaction date;
- charged amount;
- invoice or receipt;
- last four digits of the payment method;
- account email;
- evidence of account compromise where relevant.

Company may suspend the account or payment method during an investigation to reduce fraud risk.

A payment dispute submitted to a bank or card issuer does not automatically establish that the transaction was unauthorized.

### 3.4 Fraud Prevention

Company may place a transaction into a manual review queue for up to **24 hours**.

Potential fraud indicators include:

- repeated payment failures;
- multiple accounts using the same promotional benefit;
- unusual geographic or device activity;
- rapid subscription purchases and cancellations;
- mismatched billing information;
- known compromised payment instruments.

Company may refuse, delay, suspend, or terminate a transaction or subscription where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance.

### 3.5 International Customers

Availability, taxes, supported payment methods, and billing currencies may vary by country.

For international transactions:

- the checkout currency shown before purchase controls the customer-facing subscription price;
- the customer's bank or card issuer may apply foreign-exchange conversion charges;
- Company is generally not responsible for fees charged independently by the customer's bank or payment provider;
- local VAT, GST, sales tax, digital-services tax, or similar charges may be added where required.

Foreign exchange rates used by a payment processor may differ from rates shown on public currency-conversion websites.

### 3.6 Taxes

Subscription prices may be displayed exclusive or inclusive of taxes depending on jurisdiction and checkout presentation.

Where required, Company may collect:

- VAT;
- GST;
- sales or use tax;
- digital services tax; or
- other mandatory governmental charges.

Business customers seeking tax-exempt treatment must provide valid exemption documentation before the applicable invoice is finalized, where supported.

### 3.7 Standard vs. Enterprise Subscriptions

| Topic | Standard / Self-Service | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Public or checkout price | Signed order form / contract |
| Billing cycle | Monthly or annual | Contract-specific |
| Cancellation | Self-service rules | Contractual notice period |
| Refunds | Standard refund rules | Contract terms control |
| Seats | Plan limits | Contract-defined |
| Payment terms | Generally immediate payment | May be Net 15, Net 30, or as contracted |
| Currency | Checkout-supported currencies | Contract-defined |
| Support | Standard support | Contractual support/SLA |
| Renewal | Automatic where disclosed | Contract-specific |

If an Enterprise agreement conflicts with this policy, the **signed Enterprise agreement controls** to the extent permitted by law.

### 3.8 Promotional Pricing

Promotional discounts:

- apply only for the stated promotional period;
- may expire automatically;
- may be limited to eligible customers;
- generally cannot be combined with another promotion unless explicitly allowed.

After a promotional period ends, subsequent billing occurs at the then-applicable standard price unless another written offer applies.

### 3.9 Price Changes

Company may change subscription prices subject to applicable law and contractual requirements.

Where advance notice is required or contractually promised, Company will provide notice before the changed price first applies.

Unless otherwise stated, an already-paid billing period is not retrospectively repriced.

### 3.10 Account Deletion

Deleting a Company account does **not automatically guarantee cancellation of an external subscription** purchased through a third-party marketplace.

Customers must cancel the subscription through the original billing provider where that provider controls recurring billing.

## 4. Processing Times, Fees & Financial Terms

### 4.1 Standard Processing Timelines

| Action | Typical Processing Time | Maximum/Review Window |
|---|---:|---:|
| Subscription activation | Within 5 minutes | Up to 24 hours for manual review |
| Plan upgrade | Immediate after authorization | Up to 24 hours if payment is reviewed |
| Plan downgrade | Next renewal | Contract or checkout terms may differ |
| Cancellation request | Immediate confirmation | Access generally continues through paid term |
| Duplicate-charge investigation | 1–3 business days | Usually 5–7 business days |
| Approved refund initiation | 1–3 business days | Payment provider may require longer |
| Card refund posting | 5–10 business days | Issuer-dependent |
| Bank-transfer settlement | 1–5 business days | Country/bank dependent |
| Manual billing investigation | 1–3 business days | Up to 7 business days in complex cases |

### 4.2 Subscription Fees

The amount payable is the amount shown during checkout or stated in the applicable contract, together with any applicable taxes and approved charges.

Company does not generally charge a separate cancellation fee for standard self-service subscriptions.

### 4.3 Proration

Proration may apply to qualifying upgrades.

Example:

- Current monthly plan: **$30/month**
- Upgraded monthly plan: **$50/month**
- Remaining subscription period: **15 of 30 days**
- Indicative incremental charge: approximately **$10**, before taxes and subject to the billing platform's actual proration calculation.

Proration calculations may use billing-system precision rather than simple rounded daily arithmetic.

Downgrades generally do not generate a cash refund for unused time.

### 4.4 Refund Destination

Approved refunds are normally returned to the **original payment method**.

Alternative refund destinations may be unavailable because of:

- payment-network restrictions;
- fraud controls;
- regulatory requirements;
- unsupported payment methods;
- third-party marketplace rules.

### 4.5 Currency Handling

Where multiple currencies are supported, the amount charged is determined by the currency selected or displayed at checkout.

The following are outside Company's control:

- exchange-rate spreads imposed by banks;
- cross-border transaction fees;
- card issuer foreign transaction fees;
- intermediary-bank charges.

### 4.6 Invoices and Receipts

A billing receipt or invoice is normally generated after successful payment.

Business customers may need to provide:

- legal entity name;
- billing address;
- tax identification number;
- purchase-order number, if supported;
- applicable exemption documentation.

Requests to correct an invoice should normally be submitted within **30 calendar days** of issuance.

### 4.7 Chargebacks

Customers should contact Company before initiating a chargeback where the issue can reasonably be resolved through Support.

A chargeback may cause:

- temporary account restriction;
- suspension of paid access;
- additional transaction review;
- collection of evidence concerning the transaction.

Company reserves the right to contest chargebacks that it believes are invalid.

## 5. Escalations & Customer Responsibilities

### 5.1 Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

1. Keeping billing information accurate.
2. Maintaining a valid recurring payment method.
3. Reviewing renewal dates and prices.
4. Cancelling before the renewal deadline when future billing is not desired.
5. Protecting account credentials.
6. Reporting suspected unauthorized activity promptly.
7. Retaining invoices, receipts, confirmation emails, and relevant transaction identifiers.
8. Ensuring that use of the subscription complies with Company's terms and applicable law.

### 5.2 Required Documentation for Billing Disputes

For efficient investigation, Support may request:

- account email;
- subscription plan;
- transaction date;
- transaction amount;
- currency;
- invoice or receipt number;
- payment method type;
- last four digits of the card, where applicable;
- cancellation confirmation;
- screenshots showing the relevant billing state;
- Enterprise contract or order-form reference, where applicable.

Customers should **not** send full card numbers, CVVs, passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials to Support.

### 5.3 First-Level Support Escalation

A customer should first contact Support through the designated Company support channel.

The customer should clearly identify whether the request concerns:

- cancellation;
- renewal;
- duplicate billing;
- refund;
- payment failure;
- unauthorized charge;
- plan change;
- invoice correction; or
- Enterprise contractual billing.

### 5.4 Billing Operations Escalation

Support may escalate the matter to Billing Operations when:

- a payment ledger requires reconciliation;
- duplicate charging is suspected;
- a refund exception is requested;
- payment processor records differ from Company records;
- a subscription state is inconsistent with the billing record.

Billing Operations generally provides an outcome within **5–7 business days** for standard cases.

### 5.5 Enterprise Escalation

Enterprise customers should follow the escalation mechanism stated in the applicable order form, master services agreement, SLA, or account-management process.

Contractual timelines override standard customer-support targets where expressly stated.

### 5.6 Dispute Resolution

For unresolved disputes, customers should preserve all relevant documentation and reference the original support case number.

Where applicable, Company may request that a customer complete internal billing review before pursuing external dispute processes, except where the customer has a legal right to proceed directly.

### 5.7 Policy Interpretation

This policy is intended to establish consistent subscription operations. It does not limit mandatory rights granted to consumers under applicable law.

Where local law provides a stronger customer protection than this policy, the applicable legal requirement prevails.

> **Critical warning:** Cancelling an application, deleting an account, removing a payment card, or becoming inactive does **not by itself cancel a recurring subscription** unless the applicable billing system explicitly confirms cancellation.

> **Critical warning:** A cancellation confirmation should be retained. The customer's responsibility is to verify that the subscription status changed to **Canceled** or **Scheduled for Cancellation** and that a future renewal date is no longer active.

> **Critical warning:** Enterprise customers must rely primarily on their signed contract, order form, and applicable SLA where those documents contain subscription-specific terms.
