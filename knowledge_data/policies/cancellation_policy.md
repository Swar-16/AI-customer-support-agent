## 1. General Overview & Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy defines the rules, eligibility requirements, procedures, fees, timelines, and exceptions governing cancellation of Company orders, subscriptions, services, and eligible transactions.

Cancellation means a customer-requested termination of an order, subscription, service, or other eligible transaction before the applicable fulfillment, activation, renewal, or service-completion point.

Cancellation does not automatically constitute a refund. **Cancellation eligibility and refund eligibility are separate determinations.**

### 1.2 General Cancellation Rule

Customers may request cancellation through an authenticated Company account, the applicable order-management interface, or Customer Support.

Unless a product-specific or contractual rule provides a different deadline:

- Orders may be cancelled **within 24 hours of order confirmation** if fulfillment has not started.
- Subscription cancellations may be submitted **at any time before the next renewal date**.
- Cancellation requests received after fulfillment, shipment, activation, or service commencement may be rejected or converted into a return, refund, or termination request under the applicable policy.
- Cancellation requests are processed using the **timestamp recorded by Company systems**, not the time the customer began composing or sending a message.
- A cancellation is not effective until Company systems generate a cancellation confirmation or an authorized agent confirms cancellation.

### 1.3 Eligibility Requirements

A cancellation request is eligible when all applicable conditions below are satisfied:

| Requirement | Standard Rule |
|---|---|
| Account ownership | Request must originate from the account holder or an authorized representative |
| Authentication | Customer must pass applicable account verification |
| Cancellation window | Request must be submitted before the applicable deadline |
| Fulfillment status | Physical orders must not have entered irreversible fulfillment |
| Service status | Services must not have passed the applicable commencement point |
| Payment status | Transaction must be identifiable and associated with the customer account |
| Fraud review | Request must not be blocked by an active fraud/security investigation |
| Contractual terms | Enterprise or custom-contract terms take precedence where expressly agreed |

### 1.4 Cancellation Status Definitions

Company systems use the following operational statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cancellation Requested** | Customer submitted a cancellation request; review may still be required |
| **Cancellation Approved** | Company accepted the cancellation request |
| **Cancellation Rejected** | Request does not satisfy applicable cancellation conditions |
| **Cancellation Pending** | Cancellation requires additional operational or financial processing |
| **Cancellation Completed** | Cancellation has been fully processed |
| **Cancellation Reversed** | Previously requested cancellation was withdrawn or could not be completed |
| **Cancellation Escalated** | Request requires specialist review |

### 1.5 Subscription Cancellation

For recurring subscriptions:

1. Customers may cancel at any time before the next billing renewal.
2. Unless the applicable subscription terms state otherwise, cancellation prevents future renewal but does not automatically terminate the customer's current paid service period.
3. Customers generally retain access until the end of the already-paid service period.
4. A cancellation submitted after a renewal has successfully processed applies to the newly renewed period unless a separate refund exception is approved.
5. Promotional, discounted, annual, prepaid, or minimum-term subscriptions may have additional restrictions.

### 1.6 Cancellation vs. Refund

A successful cancellation does not necessarily result in money being returned.

The financial outcome depends on:

- whether payment has settled;
- whether fulfillment or service delivery has started;
- whether the item or service is refundable;
- whether a cancellation fee applies;
- whether the transaction was promotional or discounted;
- the payment method;
- applicable contractual terms; and
- applicable law.

> **Critical:** Agents must not promise a refund solely because a cancellation request is eligible.

## 2. Step-by-Step Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Customer Cancellation Procedure

The customer should complete the following steps:

1. **Sign in** to the Company account associated with the order or subscription.
2. Navigate to **Orders**, **Subscriptions**, or the relevant service-management page.
3. Select the transaction to be cancelled.
4. Select **Cancel** or **Request Cancellation**.
5. Review the displayed cancellation consequences, including:
   - cancellation deadline;
   - applicable fee;
   - refund amount, if any;
   - remaining service period;
   - loss of promotional pricing or credits, where applicable.
6. Confirm the cancellation request.
7. Record the displayed **Cancellation Request ID**.
8. Wait for the cancellation confirmation email or in-account confirmation.
9. If the interface does not permit cancellation, contact Customer Support before the applicable deadline and provide the order, subscription, or service identifier.

### 2.2 Agent Cancellation Procedure

Agents must perform the following actions:

1. **Authenticate the customer** using the approved verification procedure.
2. Locate the relevant transaction using the order ID, subscription ID, or account information.
3. Verify:
   - transaction status;
   - fulfillment status;
   - payment status;
   - cancellation deadline;
   - product/service cancellation rules;
   - applicable contractual tier.
4. Determine whether cancellation is:
   - automatically eligible;
   - eligible with a fee;
   - eligible but requires specialist approval;
   - ineligible; or
   - subject to another workflow.
5. If eligible, submit the cancellation through the authorized internal system.
6. Record the cancellation reason using the approved reason code.
7. If a refund is applicable, initiate or queue the refund according to the Refund Policy.
8. Provide the customer with:
   - cancellation status;
   - effective cancellation date;
   - cancellation reference number;
   - expected financial-processing timeline.
9. Document any exception or discretionary approval in the case record.
10. Do not disclose internal fraud-detection rules, security thresholds, or confidential risk signals.

### 2.3 Cancellation Request Before Fulfillment

For eligible physical orders:

1. Check whether fulfillment has started.
2. If fulfillment has **not started**, approve cancellation when the request is within the applicable cancellation window.
3. If fulfillment has started but shipment has not been handed to the carrier, attempt an operational cancellation.
4. If cancellation cannot be stopped operationally, advise the customer that the order may need to proceed through the applicable return/refund process.
5. Never represent an operational cancellation attempt as completed until the system confirms it.

### 2.4 Cancellation After Shipment

Once a physical order has been handed to a carrier:

- Standard cancellation is generally unavailable.
- The customer may need to accept the shipment and initiate an eligible return.
- If the shipment is refused or returned to sender, the financial outcome is determined under the applicable return/refund rules.
- Any applicable return shipping or restocking fee may be deducted where permitted.

### 2.5 Subscription Cancellation Procedure

For subscriptions:

1. Verify the subscription identifier and account ownership.
2. Confirm the next renewal date.
3. Submit cancellation before renewal where possible.
4. Confirm whether cancellation means:
   - immediate termination; or
   - termination at the end of the paid period.
5. Confirm whether recurring billing has been disabled.
6. Provide the customer with a cancellation confirmation.
7. If cancellation is submitted within **24 hours after an unintended renewal**, route the case for billing review when the subscription terms permit discretionary review.

### 2.6 Cancellation During a Technical Failure

If a customer cannot cancel because of a Company-controlled technical failure:

1. Customer Support must record the attempted cancellation time.
2. Capture the relevant order/subscription identifier and error information where available.
3. If the request was attempted before the contractual deadline, the request should be evaluated based on the **original attempted cancellation timestamp**.
4. The customer must not be disadvantaged solely because a Company system prevented timely submission.
5. Escalate unresolved cases to Customer Operations when manual cancellation is required.

## 3. Exceptions, Restrictions & Special Cases

### 3.1 Non-Cancellable Transactions

The following may be non-cancellable or subject to separate contractual rules:

- Custom-made or personalized products after production begins.
- Digital products after permitted access or download has occurred, where applicable.
- Services that have already been fully performed.
- Transactions expressly identified as **non-cancellable** before purchase.
- Promotional transactions whose terms expressly exclude cancellation.
- Certain prepaid or minimum-term enterprise services.
- Orders already irreversibly committed to fulfillment.
- Transactions restricted by law, regulation, sanctions, payment-network rules, or other mandatory requirements.

> **Warning:** "Non-refundable" and "non-cancellable" are not interchangeable terms. An item may be cancellable but non-refundable, or non-cancellable but eligible for a separate statutory remedy.

### 3.2 Customized and Made-to-Order Products

A customized order may be cancelled before production begins.

Once production has started:

- cancellation may be rejected;
- a cancellation fee may apply;
- a refund may be unavailable; or
- an exception may require management approval.

The production-start timestamp recorded by Company systems is authoritative unless evidence establishes a system error.

### 3.3 Enterprise and Business Customers

Enterprise customers are governed by their signed agreement where that agreement contains cancellation provisions.

Potential contractual terms include:

- minimum commitments;
- notice periods;
- early termination fees;
- committed annual spend;
- prepaid balances;
- service-specific termination procedures.

If an enterprise agreement conflicts with this general policy, **the signed agreement controls** for that customer.

Agents must not waive contractual termination fees without authorized approval.

### 3.4 Promotional Transactions

Promotional orders may have cancellation consequences different from standard orders.

If cancellation is permitted:

- promotional discounts may not be reinstated;
- promotional credits may be forfeited;
- bundled benefits may be recalculated;
- refund calculations may use the actual amount paid rather than the standard list price.

### 3.5 Fraud and Security Review

Company may delay, restrict, or reject a cancellation or associated refund when a transaction is under an active fraud, payment-security, account-takeover, or compliance review.

In such cases:

- the customer may be asked to complete additional verification;
- processing may be delayed;
- funds may remain temporarily restricted;
- support agents must not disclose fraud-detection logic.

### 3.6 International Transactions

International cancellations may be affected by:

- local consumer-protection requirements;
- payment-provider restrictions;
- banking holidays;
- currency-conversion rules;
- cross-border settlement;
- sanctions or compliance requirements.

Where a customer paid in a foreign currency, the refund amount is normally based on the amount authorized for refund under the applicable transaction rules. The amount ultimately displayed by the customer's bank may differ because of exchange-rate movements or bank fees.

Company does not guarantee reimbursement of third-party foreign-exchange charges unless expressly required or approved.

### 3.7 Partial Cancellation

If an order contains multiple items:

- eligible individual items may be cancelled separately;
- bundled pricing may be recalculated;
- cancellation of one component may invalidate a bundle discount;
- shipping or processing charges may become non-refundable if the remaining order falls below the applicable threshold.

### 3.8 Duplicate Cancellation Requests

Submitting multiple cancellation requests does not accelerate processing.

Where duplicate requests exist:

1. Company uses the earliest valid request timestamp.
2. Subsequent requests are associated with the original case.
3. Customers should retain the original cancellation reference.
4. Agents should avoid creating duplicate refund or cancellation transactions.

## 4. Processing Times, Fees & Financial Terms

### 4.1 Standard Cancellation Processing

| Event | Target Processing Time |
|---|---:|
| Automated cancellation request | Immediate to 15 minutes |
| Manual cancellation review | Up to 1 business day |
| Operational fulfillment-stop request | Up to 1 business day |
| Cancellation confirmation | Normally within 24 hours |
| Refund initiation after approved cancellation | Normally within 1 business day |
| Refund to card/bank | Typically **5–7 business days** after initiation |
| International refund | Typically **5–10 business days** |
| Manual exception review | Typically **3–5 business days** |

These are operational targets, not guarantees where a third-party payment processor, bank, carrier, or legal review causes additional delay.

### 4.2 Cancellation Fees

Unless a product, service, or contract specifies otherwise:

| Scenario | Cancellation Fee |
|---|---:|
| Eligible cancellation before fulfillment | **$0** |
| Eligible subscription cancellation before renewal | **$0** |
| Cancellation after fulfillment begins | May be **up to 10%** of affected order value |
| Customized product after production begins | May be **up to 25%** of affected order value |
| Contractual early termination | Per signed agreement |
| Manual exception processing | Normally **$0**, unless contract/product terms state otherwise |

All applicable fees must be disclosed before the customer confirms an eligible cancellation when the system supports advance disclosure.

### 4.3 Currency Handling

For transactions paid in currencies other than the Company's settlement currency:

- Cancellation eligibility is determined using the original transaction details.
- Any approved refund is processed in the currency supported by the original payment method where technically available.
- Exchange-rate differences may result in a different amount appearing in the customer's local currency.
- Company generally does not compensate for exchange-rate fluctuations occurring after the original transaction.

### 4.4 Payment Method Constraints

Refund/cancellation processing depends on the original payment method.

| Payment Method | Typical Financial Processing |
|---|---|
| Credit/debit card | 5–7 business days after refund initiation |
| Bank transfer | 3–7 business days |
| Digital wallet | Typically 1–5 business days |
| Company account credit | Typically same day |
| Gift/prepaid balance | Returned according to applicable instrument terms |
| Third-party financing | Subject to financing provider rules |

### 4.5 Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

Customers should contact Company before initiating a payment dispute when the issue concerns cancellation or refund.

If a chargeback is already open:

- Company may suspend ordinary refund processing for the disputed amount.
- The payment provider's dispute process may control the final outcome.
- Agents must not issue duplicate refunds for the same disputed transaction.

## 5. Escalations & Customer Responsibilities

### 5.1 Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- submitting cancellation requests before applicable deadlines;
- using the correct account;
- providing accurate order/subscription identifiers;
- retaining cancellation confirmation numbers;
- completing reasonable identity verification;
- providing evidence when disputing a cancellation decision;
- checking the original purchase terms for product-specific restrictions.

### 5.2 Required Documentation

Depending on the case, Company may request:

- order ID;
- subscription ID;
- cancellation request ID;
- payment transaction reference;
- proof of account ownership;
- screenshots of technical errors;
- relevant correspondence;
- enterprise contract or purchase-order information.

Company will request only information reasonably necessary for the applicable review.

### 5.3 Escalation Levels

| Level | Owner | Target Response |
|---|---|---:|
| Level 1 | Customer Support | Within 1 business day |
| Level 2 | Customer Operations | Within 2 business days |
| Level 3 | Billing/Payments Specialist | Within 3 business days |
| Level 4 | Legal/Compliance | Typically 5–10 business days |

Urgent escalation may be appropriate for suspected unauthorized transactions, duplicate billing, regulatory deadlines, or material enterprise-account impact.

### 5.4 Disputed Cancellation

If a customer believes a cancellation was incorrectly rejected:

1. Contact Customer Support with the cancellation/order reference.
2. State the date and approximate time of the cancellation attempt.
3. Explain why the request should qualify.
4. Provide relevant evidence, especially screenshots or confirmation messages.
5. Customer Operations reviews the transaction history.
6. If required, the case is escalated to Billing, Legal, Compliance, or the applicable specialist team.
7. The customer receives the final operational determination and any applicable remedy.

### 5.5 System Timestamp Disputes

When cancellation timing is disputed, Company reviews available records, which may include:

- authenticated account events;
- API request timestamps;
- order-state transitions;
- payment records;
- support-case timestamps;
- cancellation confirmation records.

An agent's memory or an unsaved browser session does not override recorded transaction evidence.

### 5.6 Policy Precedence

Where multiple policies apply, the following order generally governs:

1. Applicable law and mandatory regulatory requirements.
2. Signed customer contract or enterprise agreement.
3. Product/service-specific terms presented at purchase.
4. Applicable refund/return policy.
5. This Cancellation Policy.
6. General customer-support procedures.

Where a conflict is identified, agents must escalate rather than make an unsupported exception.

### 5.7 Policy Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and may be updated sooner when operational, contractual, payment-network, regulatory, or product requirements change.

The policy version and effective date shown at the top of this document identify the version applicable to the relevant cancellation event, subject to mandatory legal requirements and contractual terms.
