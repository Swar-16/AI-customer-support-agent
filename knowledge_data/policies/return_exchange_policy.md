# Return & Exchange Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Policy Owner:** Customer Experience & Operations  
**Policy Domain:** RETURN & EXCHANGE  
**Scope:** Standard consumer orders, eligible physical goods, domestic and international shipments  
**Applies To:** Customers, Customer Support, Fulfillment, Returns Operations, Finance, Fraud & Risk  
**Supersedes:** All prior return and exchange procedures for this domain

---

## 1. General Overview & Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Standard Return Window

Customers may request a return for an eligible item **within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery**.

The return request must be submitted through the Company's approved return channel before the eligibility deadline expires.

For multi-item orders, the 30-day period is calculated independently for each delivered item based on its confirmed delivery date.

### 1.2 Standard Exchange Window

Eligible items may be exchanged **within 30 calendar days of confirmed delivery**, subject to:

- Product availability.
- The replacement item being eligible for exchange.
- The original item meeting all return-condition requirements.
- The requested replacement being equal to or greater than the original item's value, where applicable.
- Any applicable price difference being paid before the replacement is dispatched.

### 1.3 Condition Requirements

Unless a product-specific policy states otherwise, returned merchandise must be:

- **Unused and unworn**.
- In saleable condition.
- Free from stains, odors, washing, alterations, damage, or misuse.
- Returned with original packaging where reasonably available.
- Returned with included accessories, manuals, tags, components, and promotional items.

Customers may inspect an item as they reasonably would in a retail environment. Normal inspection does not by itself make an item ineligible.

### 1.4 Proof of Purchase

A valid order number, receipt, invoice, or other Company-verifiable proof of purchase is required.

If the customer cannot provide an order number, Customer Support may locate the transaction using verified account information or other transaction details.

Refunds are normally issued to the **original payment method**.

### 1.5 Eligibility Summary

| Scenario | Eligible? | Standard Requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Unused item within 30 days | Yes | Meets condition requirements |
| Unused item after 30 days | No | Exception approval required |
| Used item | Generally No | Manufacturing defect may qualify |
| Damaged on arrival | Yes | Report promptly with evidence |
| Wrong item shipped | Yes | Company-paid resolution |
| Defective item | Yes | Verification may be required |
| Final-sale item | No | Unless defective, damaged, or legally required |
| Personalized/custom item | Generally No | Defect or Company error may qualify |
| Gift purchase | Yes | Refund/exchange rules depend on transaction |
| International order | Conditional | Destination and carrier restrictions apply |

### 1.6 Return Authorization Requirement

Where the return system requires authorization, the customer must obtain an **RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number** before shipping the item.

An RMA is valid for **14 calendar days from issuance** unless the authorization explicitly specifies another expiration date.

> **WARNING:** Customers must not ship returns to an address obtained from an old order, invoice, or third-party source. Unauthorized returns may experience processing delays or may be returned to the sender.

### 1.7 Item-Level Eligibility

Eligibility is determined at the item level. A customer may return one eligible item from an order while retaining other items.

Bundled products may be treated as a single return unit when the bundle was sold as a single commercial package.

## 2. Step-by-Step Operating Procedures

### 2.1 Customer Return Procedure

1. **Verify the delivery date.**
   - Confirm the item was delivered within the applicable return window.
   - Standard eligibility is **30 calendar days from confirmed delivery**.

2. **Verify item eligibility.**
   - Check whether the item is excluded from returns.
   - Confirm the item is unused, undamaged, and complete unless the return concerns a defect, shipping damage, or Company error.

3. **Submit a return request.**
   - Provide the order number.
   - Identify the item or items being returned.
   - Select the applicable reason.
   - Provide photographs or supporting documentation when requested.

4. **Obtain RMA authorization when required.**
   - Record the RMA number.
   - Do not combine unrelated RMAs unless Customer Support explicitly authorizes the consolidation.

5. **Package the merchandise.**
   - Use appropriate protective packaging.
   - Include required accessories and components.
   - Remove or cover previous shipping labels where necessary.

6. **Ship using the authorized method.**
   - Follow the return label or carrier instructions supplied by the Company.
   - Retain the tracking number until the refund or exchange is completed.

7. **Return receipt and inspection.**
   - Returns Operations records receipt.
   - Eligible returns are normally inspected within **3 business days**.

8. **Resolution.**
   - Approved refunds are initiated after inspection.
   - Approved exchanges enter fulfillment after eligibility confirmation and payment of any required price difference.

### 2.2 Agent Return Procedure

Customer Support must:

1. Authenticate the customer using approved account-verification procedures.
2. Locate the order and confirm delivery status.
3. Determine whether the item falls within the applicable return window.
4. Check product-specific restrictions.
5. Determine whether the request is:
   - Standard return.
   - Exchange.
   - Defective merchandise.
   - Damaged shipment.
   - Wrong item received.
   - Late return.
   - Fraud/risk review.
6. Explain applicable fees and refund expectations before authorization.
7. Create an RMA where required.
8. Record the return reason accurately.
9. Provide return instructions.
10. Escalate exceptions when approval authority is exceeded.

### 2.3 Damaged-on-Arrival Procedure

If an item arrives visibly damaged:

1. Customer should report the damage **within 7 calendar days of delivery**.
2. Customer Support should request:
   - Order number.
   - Description of damage.
   - Photographs of the product.
   - Photographs of external and internal packaging when available.
3. Support determines whether carrier or fulfillment investigation is required.
4. If validated, the Company may provide:
   - Replacement.
   - Exchange.
   - Full refund.
5. The Company may provide a prepaid return label where return shipment is required.

### 2.4 Defective Product Procedure

For suspected manufacturing defects:

1. Customer submits the defect description.
2. Support may request photographs, video, serial number, or diagnostic information.
3. Technical or Quality Operations may review the claim.
4. If the defect is confirmed or reasonably established, the Company may authorize:
   - Replacement.
   - Exchange.
   - Repair, where supported.
   - Refund.
5. A defective-item return is not treated as ordinary customer-remorse merchandise when the defect is attributable to the product.

### 2.5 Exchange Procedure

1. Confirm the original item is exchange-eligible.
2. Confirm the requested replacement is in stock.
3. Calculate the applicable price difference.
4. Collect any additional amount before dispatch.
5. If the replacement is cheaper, process the applicable refund after confirmation of the returned item.
6. Dispatch the replacement after the return has passed required validation, unless an approved advance-exchange program applies.
7. Provide the new shipment tracking information.

## 3. Exceptions, Restrictions & Special Cases

### 3.1 Non-Refundable and Final-Sale Merchandise

The following categories are generally **non-refundable and non-exchangeable** unless defective, damaged, incorrectly shipped, or otherwise required by applicable law:

- Final-sale merchandise.
- Clearance items explicitly designated final sale.
- Personalized or custom-made products.
- Products manufactured to customer-specific specifications.
- Certain hygiene-sensitive products after opening.
- Consumable products after opening or use.
- Gift cards or stored-value products.
- Downloadable or digitally delivered products where applicable.

Product-specific terms shown at purchase control where they impose stricter restrictions.

### 3.2 Promotional and Bundled Purchases

If a promotional discount depended on purchasing multiple items, returning one item may cause the promotion to be recalculated.

The refund may therefore be lower than the item's originally displayed standalone price.

Free promotional gifts included with an order may need to be returned when the qualifying purchase is returned.

### 3.3 Partial Returns

Partial returns are permitted when the individual item is eligible.

If a partial return causes the remaining order value to fall below a promotional threshold, the Company may recalculate discounts before determining the final refund.

### 3.4 Gifts

Gift recipients may be eligible for an exchange or merchandise credit where the original transaction qualifies.

Refunds to a gift recipient are not normally issued to a different payment method from the original transaction.

### 3.5 International Returns

International returns are subject to:

- Destination-country restrictions.
- Carrier availability.
- Customs requirements.
- Import/export documentation.
- Applicable duties and taxes.
- Product restrictions imposed by the destination country.

The Company does not guarantee that customs authorities will release a returned shipment without additional documentation.

Unless otherwise required by law or expressly stated at purchase, original international duties, taxes, customs charges, and brokerage fees may be **non-refundable**.

### 3.6 Fraud and Abuse Prevention

The Company may delay, deny, or escalate a return where there are reasonable indicators of:

- Serial return abuse.
- Payment fraud.
- Account takeover.
- Product substitution.
- Counterfeit merchandise.
- Missing or materially different components.
- Repeated claims inconsistent with transaction records.
- Unauthorized resale or commercial activity.

Fraud screening may include order history, return history, payment information, device/account signals, shipping information, and product identifiers, subject to applicable law.

> **WARNING:** Customers must return the same merchandise purchased under the applicable order. Returning a different item, counterfeit product, or materially altered product may result in denial of the return and further account review.

### 3.7 Enterprise and Commercial Accounts

Enterprise or commercial customers may operate under negotiated contractual terms.

Where a signed enterprise agreement conflicts with this policy, the **contractual return terms control** for the applicable transaction.

Standard consumer return windows do not automatically apply to wholesale, bulk, or commercial purchases.

### 3.8 Late Returns

Requests submitted after the standard 30-day period are normally denied.

Customer Support may escalate exceptional circumstances, including:

- Confirmed Company system error.
- Material shipping delay attributable to the Company or carrier.
- Product defect discovered shortly after the standard window.
- Documented exceptional circumstances approved by management.

Approval is discretionary unless applicable law provides a mandatory right.

### 3.9 Lost Return Shipments

The customer should retain carrier tracking information until the return is resolved.

If the customer used a Company-provided return label, Operations may investigate the shipment with the carrier.

If the customer independently selected a carrier, responsibility for loss or damage during transit may remain with the customer unless otherwise required by law or agreed by the Company.

## 4. Processing Times, Fees & Financial Terms

### 4.1 Standard Processing Timeline

| Stage | Target Timeline |
|---|---:|
| Return request submission | Immediate |
| RMA issuance | Usually within 1 business day |
| RMA validity | 14 calendar days |
| Carrier transit | Typically 3–7 business days |
| Return inspection | Within 3 business days of receipt |
| Refund initiation after approval | Within 2 business days |
| Bank/card posting | Typically 5–7 business days |
| Exchange fulfillment after approval | Typically 2–5 business days |

Bank, card-network, payment-provider, customs, and carrier delays may extend these estimates.

### 4.2 Return Shipping Fees

| Return Reason | Standard Shipping Treatment |
|---|---|
| Customer changed mind | Customer-paid unless otherwise stated |
| Wrong item shipped | Company-paid |
| Defective item | Company-paid where defect is validated |
| Damaged on arrival | Company-paid where validated |
| Company fulfillment error | Company-paid |
| International customer-remorse return | Customer-paid unless otherwise stated |

Shipping fees are not automatically refundable merely because the merchandise itself qualifies for a refund.

### 4.3 Restocking and Processing Fees

Standard consumer returns normally have **no restocking fee** when merchandise is returned in compliant condition.

A processing/restocking deduction of up to **15% of the merchandise value** may apply to certain commercial, custom, bulk, or contract-specific returns when disclosed in the applicable terms.

No such fee should be charged where prohibited by applicable law.

### 4.4 Refund Calculation

The refund generally consists of:

**Eligible merchandise amount**
  
minus **applicable discount recalculation**
  
minus **authorized deductions or fees**
  
plus/minus **applicable adjustments required by law or transaction terms**

The refund is normally issued in the currency of the original transaction.

### 4.5 Currency Conversion

For international transactions, refunds are normally processed for the amount authorized in the original transaction currency.

The amount appearing in the customer's bank account may differ in local currency because of:

- Exchange-rate fluctuations.
- Issuer conversion rates.
- Foreign transaction fees.
- Bank fees.

The Company does not generally reimburse fees charged independently by a customer's bank or card issuer.

### 4.6 Exchanges and Price Differences

If the replacement item costs more:

- Customer pays the difference before dispatch.

If the replacement costs less:

- The difference is refunded to the original payment method after exchange eligibility is confirmed.

Promotional pricing may be recalculated based on the terms of the original purchase.

### 4.7 Refund Status

If the Company confirms that a refund was issued but the customer has not received it after **7 business days**, Customer Support should request:

- Refund confirmation/reference number.
- Payment method details sufficient for verification.
- Customer bank/card confirmation where appropriate.

Support may then escalate to Finance or Payments Operations.

## 5. Escalations & Customer Responsibilities

### 5.1 Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- Submitting requests within applicable deadlines.
- Providing accurate order and product information.
- Following packaging and carrier instructions.
- Returning the correct item.
- Retaining tracking information.
- Providing requested photographs or documentation.
- Paying applicable return shipping or price differences where required.
- Notifying Support promptly when tracking indicates a delivery exception.

### 5.2 Required Documentation

Depending on the case, Support may request:

- Order number.
- Purchase receipt/invoice.
- Photographs of product condition.
- Photographs of shipping packaging.
- Product serial number.
- Carrier tracking number.
- RMA number.
- Description of defect or damage.
- Customs documentation for international returns.

### 5.3 Escalation Levels

| Level | Trigger | Owner | Target |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Level 1 | Standard eligibility question | Customer Support | Same business day |
| Level 2 | Late return or policy exception | Support Lead | 1 business day |
| Level 3 | Fraud/risk concern | Fraud & Risk | 2 business days |
| Level 3 | Financial/refund discrepancy | Finance/Payments | 2 business days |
| Level 3 | International customs issue | International Operations | 3 business days |
| Level 4 | Legal/regulatory complaint | Legal/Compliance | 5 business days |

### 5.4 Disputed Return Decision

If a customer disputes a denied return:

1. Customer requests review through Customer Support.
2. Support verifies the original decision and evidence.
3. A Support Lead reviews cases involving ordinary policy exceptions.
4. Legal/Compliance review may be requested where the dispute raises a statutory or regulatory issue.
5. The Company communicates the final determination and applicable next steps.

### 5.5 Policy Interpretation

This policy establishes the Company's standard operational framework. It does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be waived under applicable consumer-protection, warranty, payment, or other mandatory laws.

Where mandatory law provides a more favorable customer right, the legally required right controls.

### 5.6 Audit and Record Retention

Return transactions, approvals, denials, refunds, RMAs, and material customer communications should be recorded in the Company's systems.

Operational records should normally be retained for at least **24 months**, or longer where required by law, contractual obligations, financial controls, or an active dispute.
